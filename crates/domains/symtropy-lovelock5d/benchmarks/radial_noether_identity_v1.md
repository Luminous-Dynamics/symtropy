# 5D EGB radial Noether identity and spin-squared constraint

**Status:** the off-shell identity is exactly verified from the unfixed reduced action, with the Gauss–Bonnet coupling kept symbolic.

For the radial metric component \(g_{rr}=1/f(r)\), an infinitesimal radial reparameterization generated by \(\xi(r)\) acts as
\[
\delta b=\xi b',\quad \delta g=\xi g',\quad
\delta h=\xi h',\quad \delta w=\xi w',\quad
\delta f=\xi f'-2f\xi'.
\]
The last term is essential: \(f\) is the inverse radial metric component, not a scalar.

Writing \(E_q=\partial L/\partial q-d(\partial L/\partial q')/dr\), radial diffeomorphism invariance gives
\[
\boxed{E_b b'+E_f f'+E_g g'+E_h h'+E_w w'
+2\frac{d}{dr}(fE_f)=0.}
\]
The committed SymPy replay reconstructs all five Euler–Lagrange equations from the 2010 reduced action and verifies that the expression cancels exactly for symbolic \(\alpha\).

After imposing \(g=r^2\), this becomes
\[
2rE_g+b'E_b+f'E_f+h'E_h+w'E_w+2(fE_f)'=0.
\]

For the spin expansion, the \(O(a^2)\) identity is
\[
2rE_g^{(2)}+F'E_b^{(2)}+F'E_f^{(2)}
+2rE_h^{(2)}+W_1'E_w^{(1)}
+2(FE_f^{(2)})'=0.
\]
The angular term may be removed only after \(E_w^{(1)}=0\) is independently verified.

**Consequence:** \(E_g^{(2)}\) is dependent on the other residuals in the exact continuum when the first-order angular equation holds. That provides a principled route to reduce the solver's equation count, but \(E_g\) must remain an independently evaluated qualification residual.
