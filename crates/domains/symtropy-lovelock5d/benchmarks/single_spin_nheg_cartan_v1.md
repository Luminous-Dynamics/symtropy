# Cartan-frame replay of the singly rotating 5D EGB near-horizon geometry

**Owner:** [#6389 BH-5D-EGB-SINGLE-SPIN-CARTAN-001](https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symtropy/issues/6389)

**Status:** independent coframe, Levi-Civita connection, curvature 2-forms, Ricci contractions and Kretschmann replay. The full EGB/Lanczos field equations have **not** yet been independently evaluated.

## 1. Why use Cartan geometry

The source's coordinate metric contains a nontrivial \(t\)-\(\psi\) block and singular-looking angular coordinate factors near \(\theta=\pi/2\). A direct dense coordinate-basis calculation expands many terms that are artifacts of the frame. The orthonormal coframe reduces the differential geometry to three scalar structure functions and their single nonzero frame derivatives.

Source metric: Çelik, Hajian & Kunz, *An Exact Single-Rotating Near-Horizon Geometry in Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet Gravity*, arXiv:2605.15005, Eq. (4). The source coupling is \(R+\alpha_{NH}{\cal L}_{GB}\); Symtropy uses \(R+\alpha_{sym}{\cal L}_{GB}/4\), with \(\alpha_{sym}=4\alpha_{NH}\).

Set
\[
p=1+\beta\cos^4\theta,\quad q=1-\beta\cos^4\theta,\quad
\beta=\frac{2a^2}{\alpha_{sym}},\quad 0<\beta<1.
\]
Define
\[
A=\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{sym}p}}{2\sqrt2\cos\theta},\quad
B=2A\frac qp,\quad C=a\tan\theta.
\]
The orthonormal coframe is
\[
e^0=Ar\,dt,\quad e^1=A\frac{dr}{r},\quad
e^2=B\,d\theta,\quad e^3=C\,d\varphi,\quad
e^4=2A\,d\psi+e^0,
\]
with
\[
ds^2=-(e^0)^2+(e^1)^2+(e^2)^2+(e^3)^2+(e^4)^2.
\]

This reconstructs the source line element exactly:
\[
A^2=\frac H4,\quad B^2=H\frac{q^2}{p^2},\quad
C^2=a^2\tan^2\theta,\qquad H=\frac{\Delta_+}{\cos^2\theta}.
\]
In particular, the \(dt\,d\psi\) term is generated by the \(e^4\) square and the \(dt^2\) pieces cancel between \(-(e^0)^2\) and \((e^4)^2\).

## 2. Reduced exterior algebra

Define
\[
x=\frac1A,\qquad y=\frac{A_\theta}{AB},\qquad
z=\frac{C_\theta}{BC}.
\]
The exact expressions are
\[
x=\frac{2\sqrt2\cos\theta}{\sqrt{\alpha_{sym}p}},\quad
y=\frac{x}{2}\tan\theta,\quad
z=\frac{xp}{2q\sin\theta\cos\theta}.
\]
Only \(E_2=B^{-1}\partial_\theta\) differentiates these structure functions. Write \(x_2=E_2(x)\), etc. A useful exact identity is
\[
\boxed{x_2=-xy}.
\]
The complete Cartan structure equations are
\[
\begin{aligned}
de^0&=x\,e^1\wedge e^0+y\,e^2\wedge e^0,\\
de^1&=y\,e^2\wedge e^1,\\
de^2&=0,\\
de^3&=z\,e^2\wedge e^3,\\
de^4&=y\,e^2\wedge e^4+x\,e^1\wedge e^0.
\end{aligned}
\]
The replay checks \(d^2e^a=0\) after imposing the exact \(x_2=-xy\) identity.

## 3. Levi-Civita connection and curvature

The connection is constructed from the structure coefficients using the Lorentzian Koszul formula, not copied from the paper. The resulting connection has 16 nonzero frame coefficients / 14 nonzero connection one-forms. It passes:
- zero torsion \(de^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge e^b=0\);
- metric compatibility \(\omega_{ab}+\omega_{ba}=0\);
- differential Bianchi identity \(D\Omega^a{}_b=0\).

Curvature two-forms are then evaluated from
\[
\Omega^a{}_b=d\omega^a{}_b+\omega^a{}_c\wedge\omega^c{}_b.
\]
There are 20 nonzero curvature two-form pairs. After \(x_2=-xy\), the Riemann tensor has 40 nonzero frame components, and the Ricci tensor is diagonal. The Ricci components and all nonzero connection/curvature forms are frozen in the machine-readable Cartan fixture.

A compact expression for the Kretschmann scalar in terms of the three frame functions is
\[
\begin{aligned}
K=\frac14\big(&3x^4+24x^2y^2+96y^4+96y^2y_2\\
&+48y^2z^2+48y_2^2+16z^4+32z^2z_2+16z_2^2\big).
\end{aligned}
\]

## 4. Independent recovery of the source curvature invariant

Writing \(c=\cos^2\theta\), the coframe derivatives reduce to rational functions:
\[
x^2=\frac{8c}{\alpha_{sym}(1+\beta c^2)},\quad
y^2=\frac{2(1-c)}{\alpha_{sym}(1+\beta c^2)},
\]
\[
z^2=\frac{2(1+\beta c^2)}
{\alpha_{sym}(1-\beta c^2)^2(1-c)},
\]
\[
y_2=\frac{2c(1+2\beta c-\beta c^2)}
{\alpha_{sym}(1+\beta c^2)(1-\beta c^2)},
\]
\[
z_2=-\frac{2c(-3\beta^2c^4+2\beta^2c^3+6\beta c-6\beta c^2+1)}
{\alpha_{sym}(1-\beta c^2)^3(1-c)}.
\]
Substitution into the Cartan contraction gives exactly
\[
K=
32\frac{
9\beta^6u^6+12\beta^5u^5+75\beta^4u^4+
24\beta^3u^3+23\beta^2u^2-20\beta u+5}
{\alpha_{sym}^2(\beta u-1)^6(\beta u+1)^2},
\qquad u=\cos^4\theta.
\]
This equals the source's Eq. (10) after the coupling map, and independently recovers
\[
\boxed{\lim_{\theta\to\pi/2}K=\frac{160}{\alpha_{sym}^2}}.
\]
