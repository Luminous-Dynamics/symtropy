# EXO-10 narrative experience boundary

`NarrativeExperience` is a read-only presentation projection, not world truth.

The owning simulation derives bounded inputs such as threat, uncertainty, agency, strain, resource pressure, protection instability, memory resonance, social connection, loss, and causal significance. The experience projector may smooth those inputs but may not mutate health, protection, resources, memory, relationships, AI intent, or world state.

The Muse adapter maps only semantically appropriate presentation controls (`arousal`, `valence`, and `prediction_error`). It deliberately leaves neurotransmitter-named and consciousness fields at their Muse defaults rather than pretending gameplay state measures biology or consciousness.

Memory/history resonance remains separately typed for future motif-recall integration.
