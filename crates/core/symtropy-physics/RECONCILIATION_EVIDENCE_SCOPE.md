# Reconciliation Evidence Scope

Checked reconciliation evidence validates the integrity of a stored audit and the agreement between represented measured reservoirs and the causal ledger.

It does not certify omitted physical reservoirs, upgrade approximate rigid-body energy formulas, prove lifecycle provenance that was not recorded, or authorize semantic/adaptive systems to change fidelity.

A checked `fully_reconciled` result is therefore one necessary component of authoritative physical evidence, not a universal certificate of physical exactness.
