# Plasma-assisted conductive-path transfer v0.1

**Status:** literature and modelling protocol; no physical test is authorized.  
**Parent:** [Power Fabric transport primitives #5832](https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symtropy/issues/5832)  
**Issue:** [PLASMA-CHANNEL-TRANSFER-001 #5881](https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symtropy/issues/5881)  
**Reuses:** SPECTRA #3374/#3396, optical LPT #3458, WPT empirical envelope #3448 and energy accounting #3472.

## 1. Research question

Can a laser- or field-generated plasma channel deliver a useful, measured electrical service with a net advantage over a cable, discharge path, near-field WPT or radiative power-beaming system after charging the complete energy cost of channel formation, sustaining, control, conversion and cooling?

A plasma filament is an ionized medium. A laser-created channel that guides a discharge is best classified as a **transient conductive path**, not as a new field-only transmission carrier. Its usefulness for lightning research, switching or discharge guidance does not establish an efficient continuous power-distribution system.

## 2. Source-grounded starting points

- Théberge et al. (2017), *Laser-guided energetic discharges over large air gaps by electric-field enhanced plasma filaments*, Scientific Reports: https://www.nature.com/articles/srep40063. Reports a laser-guided 30 kV discharge over a 2 m air gap after externally stimulated channel formation.
- *Long-lived laser-induced arc discharges for energy channeling applications* (2017), Scientific Reports: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-14054-z. Reports a channel lifetime up to 30 ms using a sustaining 100 mA current pulse and Joule heating.
- Papeer et al. (2019), *Towards Remote Lightning Manipulation by Meters-long Plasma Channels Generated by Ultra-Short-Pulse High-Intensity Lasers*, Scientific Reports: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6344589/. Reports a 13 m segmented “broken-wire” plasma filament in a high-intensity laser experiment.
- *Quasi-steady-state air plasma channel produced by a femtosecond laser pulse sequence* (2015), Scientific Reports: https://www.nature.com/articles/srep15515. Distinguishes short-lived single-pulse channels from pulse-sequence channels with longer measured lifetime.

These sources establish channel formation, discharge guidance or controlled channel lifetime under their experimental conditions. They do not, by themselves, establish source-electricity-to-useful-load efficiency or net energy advantage.

## 3. Physical models and classifications

Keep the following lanes separate:

1. Laser-induced ionization and conductive filaments.
2. Externally sustained arcs and transient discharge paths.
3. Segmented / “broken-wire” filament chains versus continuous channels.
4. Plasma-wave phenomena such as Langmuir modes, which require a plasma medium and are not a new vacuum scalar-wave channel.
5. Plasma-assisted RF emission/absorption and antenna effects.
6. MHD or plasma conversion, which belongs to energy conversion rather than a new transport category.

Use an explicitly regime-qualified reduced model for ionization/recombination, time-varying conductivity, Joule heating, circuit discharge and geometry. Reuse the existing SPECTRA model-selection and verification architecture. Do not invent a new general-purpose EM solver or treat the channel as a static perfect conductor.

## 4. Complete energy boundary

The total source boundary must include:

- electrical input to laser, pulse-forming or excitation equipment;
- optical pulse energy and laser conversion overhead;
- channel initiation and sustaining energy;
- external AC/DC field or bias supplies;
- discharge-driver input and control/measurement overhead;
- cooling and auxiliary services;
- energy stored in electric/magnetic fields and its change over the measurement window;
- measured useful energy delivered to the declared load.

For a matched interval, preserve:

~~~text
E_source,total = E_useful,load + E_channel/loss + E_auxiliary
                 + Delta(E_stored) + E_residual
~~~

The residual is reported, never forced to zero. Unknown source overhead blocks an efficiency claim. Peak voltage, discharge current, channel length or optical pulse energy is not a substitute for measured useful load energy.

## 5. Matched benchmark tournament

Compare candidate systems only on the same useful service, route/distance, load, operating duration, environment, duty cycle, interruption tolerance and safety envelope:

- a physical conductor/cable;
- conventional spark-gap or discharge guidance where the task is a short discharge;
- near-field inductive/capacitive WPT for a compatible nearby load;
- RF/optical beaming where the actual task is remote energy delivery;
- plasma-assisted conductive channel.

Report a vector, not a single “efficiency” score:

- source joules per useful load joule and useful service per source joule;
- continuous and pulse power at exact measurement planes;
- channel length, conductivity versus time, lifetime and continuity;
- channel formation/sustainment latency and energy overhead;
- delivered-pulse waveform, load energy and energy quality;
- repeatability, shot-to-shot path deviation, recovery and availability;
- thermal, electrode, material and maintenance burden;
- uncertainty, metrology, energy-balance residual and safety conditions.

Separate transient discharge-guidance performance from continuous power-transfer capability. A channel can win a discharge-guidance task and lose the energy-delivery task.

## 6. Synthetic-first verification

Before any physical work, build known-truth cases for:

- channel formation cost included versus omitted;
- channel conductivity decay and sustaining energy;
- segmented versus continuous path;
- load energy measured versus inferred from voltage/current peak;
- laser wall-plug overhead unknown;
- missing auxiliary supply or unsynchronized source/load meters;
- model treating time-varying plasma as a perfect conductor;
- cable baseline measured at different duty cycle or boundary;
- discharge-guidance success with negligible useful net load energy;
- unresolved channel state or invalid operating regime.

The evaluator must preserve NOT_EVALUABLE where the input evidence cannot support the requested claim.

## 7. Decision gates

- No plasma transfer primitive is admitted to the deployable Power Fabric graph without net useful service and complete source-energy cost.
- Every input and output is measured at a declared plane or remains unknown.
- Model output must reproduce source-grounded channel lifetime/conductivity observations and appropriate limiting cases before system extrapolation.
- No superiority claim against cables, WPT or optical beaming without matched service and equivalent whole-system boundaries.
- If only discharge-guidance advantage is demonstrated, classify it as a specialized discharge-control technology—not a general wireless power network.
- Plasma waves remain physically distinct from the Puharich/Tesla scalar-wave hypothesis audit.
- All high-voltage, high-intensity laser, plasma, lightning and hazardous-energy physical work requires a separately approved specialist-laboratory protocol; this document authorizes none.

## 8. Claim ceiling

This document supports source review, modelling and synthetic comparison only. It establishes no deployable plasma-transfer system, efficiency result, lightning-control capability, safety qualification or SOTA advantage.
