Synthetic lunar seed-base utility benchmark design basis, version 0.1.
Purpose: exercise a deterministic multi-interval power dispatch and batch ISRU mass-balance model.
All numerical interval values and regolith volatile fractions in the paired case are synthetic test inputs, not lunar site measurements or hardware specifications.
Critical service must be dispatched before deferrable resource-processing batches.
The reserve-aware policy uses the forecast horizon to preserve energy for declared future critical demand, subject to storage capacity, charge/discharge limits, and declared conversion efficiencies.
A batch processes its declared feed mass only if the complete batch energy demand is met; skipped batches remain unprocessed inventory.
Every interval must independently balance source energy, delivered loads, battery state, charge/discharge losses, and curtailment.
Every completed batch must independently balance feed mass into tailings, recovered water, and unrecovered contained water.
References for subsystem boundaries: NASA Moon Base Systems, NASA Lunar Surface Technology, NASA Overview of In-Situ Resource Utilization.
