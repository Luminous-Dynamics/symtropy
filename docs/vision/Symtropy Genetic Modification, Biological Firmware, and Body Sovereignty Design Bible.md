# Symtropy Genetic Modification, Biological Firmware, and Body Sovereignty Design Bible

## Version 0.1 — The Body as Commons, Code, and Kin

## Core Thesis

Genetic modification in Symtropy is not cosmetic.

It is not a simple upgrade tree.

It is not a magic path to superhumanity.

It is the political question of who may alter, own, maintain, inherit, repair, or revoke the living body.

```text
DNA is biological source code.
But the body is not merely code.
It is memory, metabolism, consent, kinship, labor, hunger, grief, and risk.
```

Genetic modification should always ask:

```text
Who owns the body?
Who maintains the body?
Who pays the metabolic cost?
Who inherits the modification?
Who can revoke access?
Who witnesses consent?
Who repairs harm?
What happens when the modified body dies?
```

Core fantasy:

```text
The flesh becomes infrastructure.
The body becomes a treaty.
```

Core conflict:

```text
adaptation versus ownership
```

Primary rule:

```text
No modification is free.
Every body has thermodynamics.
Every edit has social consequences.
```

---

# 1. Design Principles

## 1.1 No Superpower Logic

Avoid:

```text
gene mod gives fire breath
gene mod gives instant strength
gene mod gives perfect night vision with no cost
gene mod makes character universally better
```

Use:

```text
gene mod changes metabolism
gene mod changes dependency
gene mod changes diet
gene mod changes care requirements
gene mod changes civic status
gene mod changes environmental fit
gene mod changes how others read the body
```

A modification should always create a new vulnerability, obligation, or relationship.

Example:

```text
Radiation-filtering organs reduce external suit dependency,
but require rare mineral cofactors and raise water demand.
```

---

## 1.2 Biology Is Maintenance

A modified body is not “installed and complete.”

It must be maintained through:

```text
nutrition
rest
microbial balance
immune tolerance
trace minerals
repair enzymes
heat regulation
water availability
care monitoring
stable expression patterns
```

Core rule:

```text
The body is not a weapon platform.
The body is a living maintenance contract.
```

---

## 1.3 Ownership Is the Horror

The main horror of genemod politics is not mutation.

It is enclosure.

Dangerous systems include:

```text
licensed gene expression
proprietary enzyme dependency
subscription organs
encrypted phenotype records
locked reproductive inheritance
corporate clone templates
debt-bound medical maintenance
```

Core rule:

```text
A body that requires permission to keep functioning is not free.
```

---

## 1.4 Open Does Not Always Mean Safe

Open-source biological modification can be liberatory.

It can also be reckless.

Risks include:

```text
unstable expression
ecological contamination
intergenerational uncertainty
identity drift
immune collapse
unverified symbiosis
trophic dependency
community-level mutation cascades
```

Core rule:

```text
The commons must be open enough to liberate bodies,
and careful enough not to abandon them.
```

---

## 1.5 The Body Is Never Neutral

In Symtropy, every environment makes some bodies easy and others expensive.

High-gravity worlds, methane moons, radiation belts, deserts, flood basins, deep oceans, and orbital habitats all create different bodily politics.

The central question is:

```text
Who counts as adapted?
Who counts as hazardous?
Who decides?
```

# 4. Field Deck Biological Registry

The Field Deck can read heavily modified biological systems through DIAG mode, but this must be ethically framed.

It should not casually expose private medical details.

Modes:

```text
SCAN — visible body state and environmental fit
DIAG — health, stress, dependency, metabolic risk
CIVIC — consent status, rights conflicts, registry claims
ARCHIVE — phenotype history and witnessed modifications
NULL — unstable recursion, dead protocol, identity drift, mimicry
REPAIR — safe care paths and required witnesses
WITNESS — records consent, refusal, harm, treatment, restoration
```

Privacy rule:

```text
A body is not public data by default.
```

Field Deck warning:

```sh
BIOLOGICAL QUERY REQUESTED

SUBJECT: LIVING PERSON
DATA CLASS: INTIMATE
WITNESS REQUIRED: YES
CONSENT REQUIRED: YES

PROCEED ONLY IF:
- emergency condition exists
- subject consents
- care authority is present
- rights-floor exception applies
```

# 6. Clone Reconstitution and Bio-Snapshot Rights

## 6.1 Core Question

When a modified person dies and is reconstituted, should their body return with modifications intact?

Answer:

```text
Only if the modification was consented, witnessed, and preserved in a valid Bio-Snapshot.
```

The system should not use a single universal answer.

It should depend on law, faction, infrastructure, and consent.

---

## 6.2 Bio-Snapshot

A Bio-Snapshot is a recorded phenotype continuity file.

It may include:

```text
baseline genome
somatic modifications
symbiotic dependencies
medical adaptations
prosthetic integration patterns
immune tolerance profile
metabolic requirements
care instructions
consent history
inheritability restrictions
```

Field Deck path:

```sh
/dev/sym/anatomy/snapshots/agent_c/latest
```

Chronicle event:

```text
BioSnapshotWitnessed
```

Core rule:

```text
A body cannot be restored ethically without remembering what the person consented to become.
```

---

## 6.4 Best Default for Symtropy

The best default is:

```text
A player’s customized DNA strains are preserved only if they are part of a witnessed Bio-Snapshot.
```

Unwitnessed modifications become recoverable quests, not automatic losses.

Possible outcomes:

```text
stable witnessed mods restore automatically
unstable open-source mods restore in dormant form
proprietary mods require contract challenge or license replacement
alien symbioses require ecological recontact
dangerous mods require public review
Red Bloom mods may partially regrow with consequences
```

Core rule:

```text
Death should not erase identity casually.
But restoration should not ignore biological risk.
```

# 9. Null-Ecology and Morphological Drift

It is biological process detached from living meaning.



# Final Principle

Genetic modification in Symtropy should not ask:

```text
How powerful can the body become?
```

It should ask:

```text
Who gets to remain themselves while changing enough to survive?
```

The body is not property.

The body is not a product.

The body is not a weapon.

The body is a living claim on care.
