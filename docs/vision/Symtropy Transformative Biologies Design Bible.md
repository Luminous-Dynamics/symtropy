# Symtropy Transformative Biologies Design Bible

## Version 0.2 — Living Firmware, Consent, and Personhood Boundaries

## Core Thesis

Transformative Biologies are organisms, symbiotes, parasites, repair ecologies, and living firmware systems that alter the bodies of humans, animals, machines, and nonhuman species.

They are not magic.

They are not simple monsters.

They are not cosmetic mutations.

They are living systems that rewrite metabolism, sensation, movement, memory, immunity, social status, ecological belonging, and civic rights.

```text
Transformation is not the horror.

Unwitnessed, irreversible, coerced, or uncared-for transformation is the horror.
```

The central question is:

```text
Who gets to remain themselves while changing enough to survive?
```

---

# 1. Design Guardrails

## 1.1 No Real-World Bioengineering Instructions

Transformative Biologies must remain fictional, systemic, and non-procedural.

Do not depict:

```text
real genetic engineering protocols
real pathogen design
real culturing steps
real delivery mechanisms
real-world lab recipes
actionable biological harm
```

Use abstracted fictional systems:

```text
living firmware
bio-consent registries
symbiotic maintenance loops
metabolic invoices
Field Deck diagnostics
Chronicle witness records
trophic drift warnings
```

---

## 1.2 No Infection Equals Moral Corruption

A transformed person is not automatically evil.

A transformed person may be:

```text
altered
dependent
dangerous under some conditions
owed care
owed boundaries
owed witness
owed appeal
still continuous with themselves
```

The horror is not difference.

The horror is loss of consent, care, meaning, or personhood.

---

## 1.3 Separate Body Activity From Personhood

The Field Deck must distinguish:

```text
metabolic activity
motor activity
reflex activity
memory response
consent response
personhood continuity
repairability
```

A body can be alive without being autonomous.

A motor loop can continue after cognition is gone.

A symbiote can keep tissue functioning without restoring a person.

This distinction is sacred to the design.

---

## 1.4 No Weaponized Corpses

Null-Ecology Reanimated Bodies may be diagnosed, stabilized, restrained, interrupted, or allowed to rest.

They must not become collectible combat assets.

The player cannot ethically “hack a corpse” into a squad defender.

Allowed outcomes:

```text
shutdown rogue loop
redirect away from civilians
open containment path
restore memorial identity
recover testimony
preserve tissue for family or legal witness
record the failure
```

Forbidden outcome:

```text
turn cognitively extinct body into owned weapon
```

Core rule:

```text
A body without personhood is still not property.
```

# 3. Signature Transformative Biologies

## 3.1 Hemal Covenant Organism

Common misread:

```text
sci-fi vampire
```

Actual definition:

```text
A blood-sharing symbiote that transfers metabolic factors, immune information, stress hormones, memory-bearing proteins, and survival chemistry between consenting bodies.
```

Core fantasy:

```text
Blood is memory, medicine, debt, and covenant.
```

Benefits:

```text
rapid stabilization
immune sharing
radiation recovery
trauma buffering
cold survival
metabolic emergency support
```

Costs:

```text
donor dependency
compatibility law
consent burden
immune imprinting
metabolic debt
blood access politics
```

Failure mode:

```text
voluntary covenant becomes predatory dependency.
```

Core conflict:

```text
Is blood-sharing care, intimacy, labor, medicine, or coercion?
```

Field Deck warning:

```sh
HEMAL LINK REQUEST DETECTED

DATA CLASS: INTIMATE BIOLOGICAL EXCHANGE
CONSENT REQUIRED: BOTH PARTIES
DONOR BURDEN: HIGH
DEPENDENCY RISK: MODERATE
COERCION SCREEN: REQUIRED
```

Design rule:

```text
Never frame feeding as glamorous domination.
Frame it as dangerous intimacy requiring law, trust, and care.
```

---

## 3.2 Metabolic Debt-Leech

Common misread:

```text
vampire predator
```

Actual definition:

```text
A catastrophic metabolic dependency state caused by corrupted life-extension firmware, proprietary organ failure, or predatory biological maintenance systems.
```

Pathology:

```text
baseline cellular energy production collapses.
The host becomes dependent on concentrated biological energy sources.
```

Symptoms:

```text
extreme hunger
light sensitivity
blood chemistry instability
aggression during metabolic crash
cognitive narrowing under starvation
shame and secrecy
```

Gameplay:

```text
stabilize without enabling predation
find ethical donor systems
challenge corporate maintenance lock
restore ATP pathway if possible
record coercive origin
```

Failure mode:

```text
the person becomes socially treated as a monster before anyone audits who caused the dependency.
```

Core conflict:

```text
personal danger versus corporate culpability
```

---

## 3.3 Null-Ecology Reanimator

Common misread:

```text
sci-fi zombie
```

Actual definition:

```text
A failed biological repair loop that continues operating tissue after personhood has ended or become unreachable.
```

Possible causes:

```text
battlefield medical symbiote unable to stop
Red Bloom over-repair
corporate revival patch collapse
Null-Security neuromuscular hijack
failed clone restoration
automated evacuation protocol bound to dead tissue
```

Field Deck forensic readout:

```sh
$ read /dev/sym/bio/hosts/target_77

TRUTH_LAYER: LOCAL_REAL_TIME_VERIFIED

METABOLIC_TRAIT: ACTIVE
MOTOR_LOOP: RUNNING
PERSONHOOD_SIGNAL: COGNITIVE_DEAD
CONSENT_BITMASK: 0x0000
ACTIVE ROUTINE: MEDICAL_SYMBIOTE_AUTO_RECOVER_V4

DIAGNOSTIC CRITIQUE:
The original persona has crossed the threshold of cognitive extinction.
The internal repair organism has entered an infinite recovery loop.
The body is moving because the care protocol cannot stop caring.
```

Allowed player actions:

```text
observe
scan
stabilize area
interrupt motor loop
shutdown repair organism
record identity
notify kin or archive witness
prevent desecration
```

Forbidden player action:

```text
convert the body into a combat ally
```

Core conflict:

```text
When does stopping movement become mercy?
```

Design rule:

```text
A reanimated body is a tragedy, not loot.
```

# 4. Cryptographic Consent Registry

## 4.1 Definition

Every player and major NPC has a fictional, auditable Consent Registry.

This registry does not expose private medical details by default.

It stores consent states for:

```text
biological scans
symbiotic links
medical interventions
genetic modifications
inheritable edits
clone restoration
data sharing
testimony extraction
emergency exceptions
```

Core rule:

```text
Consent is not flavor text.
Consent is game state.
```


# 8. HUD Reveal Policy

## 8.1 Recommended Hybrid Model

When an undocumented organism begins interacting with the player, the HUD should first show subtle diegetic symptoms:

```text
organic phosphor blur
breathing UI pulse
miscolored diagnostic edges
slight delay in Field Deck mode switching
unusual haptic vibration
SCAN reticle blooming like tissue
```

Then, after manual DIAG or acute danger, the Field Deck must provide clear readable warnings:

```text
UNDOCUMENTED SYMBIOSIS DETECTED
METABOLIC COST RISING
NEURAL INTERFACE INTERFERENCE POSSIBLE
CONSENT STATUS: UNWITNESSED
RUN FULL DIAGNOSTIC?
```

Accessibility rule:

```text
Never make the UI unreadable as the only clue.
Always provide an accessibility-safe clear warning option.
```

Best implementation:

```text
Default mode:
subtle anomaly first, clear DIAG after player action.

Accessibility mode:
clear anomaly icon immediately, optional sensory distortion.

High-risk mode:
clear warning immediately.
```

Core principle:

```text
Mystery is good.
Unfair opacity is not.
```

# Final Principle

Transformative Biologies should not ask:

```text
How monstrous can a body become?
```

They should ask:

```text
What does care require when survival changes the body?
```

A changed body is not a failed body.

A dependent body is not a lesser body.

A dangerous body is not automatically a guilty body.

The body is a living claim on care, boundary, memory, and consent.
