# EV factory job-lifecycle evidence ledger v0.1

**Benchmark:** EV-FACTORY-TRACE-003  
**Issue:** https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symtropy/issues/2465  
**Status:** synthetic simulator-native ledger; not measured plant evidence

## Why it exists

The simulator previously emitted aggregate good-unit throughput, scrap/rework counters, station state fractions and buffer diagnostics, but no per-job release/completion history. That means an end-to-end lead-time percentile or first-pass-yield ratio could not be reconstructed from source events. The evidence-aware KPI contract correctly withheld these metrics. This version makes the simulator retain the lineage required to compute them.

## Ledger boundary

Every ledger event carries:
- schema version;
- simulation seed and scenario ID;
- monotonically increasing event sequence and simulation timestamp in seconds;
- event kind;
- pseudonymous numeric job ID and product-variant ID;
- optional station, synthetic resource, buffer, and attempt identifiers;
- evidence class set to synthetic.

The ledger currently serializes detailed events only for the **primary-seed baseline scenario** to avoid multiplying report size by every intervention and replication. The simulator still computes per-job lifecycle summaries for each seed and scenario. Reports for added-server scenarios and non-primary seeds intentionally contain no detailed event ledger; their summaries remain available for paired analysis. Each event stream is isolated by scenario and seed.

## Events and invariants

The sequence covers job release, source/interstation buffer enter/exit, station start, station end, quality pass/fail, rework start, scrap, final-good completion, and right-censoring at the horizon.

For a fixed catalog and seed:
- event sequence numbers start at zero and increase by one in deterministic record order;
- timestamps are nondecreasing;
- every released job has exactly one terminal event: final-good completion, scrap, or horizon censoring;
- each job/station has one first-attempt start and at most one observed first-attempt outcome;
- attempt numbers increase through rework;
- terminal job timestamps cannot precede job release;
- the existing output/input conservation, finite-buffer and station-time invariants remain mandatory.

A ledger may show a quality pass at the final station before final-good completion if the job is blocked waiting for the final output transfer. The final-good timestamp is recorded only when the completed job is actually accepted at the line-output boundary, not merely when operation time ends.

## Cohort and KPI semantics

- **Eligible release cohort:** jobs released at or after the configured warm-up boundary.
- **Good-job lead time:** final-good transfer timestamp minus job release timestamp, for eligible jobs accepted before the simulation horizon.
- **Right-censored at horizon:** eligible jobs not terminal at the horizon. They are counted separately, never imputed as completed jobs or assigned an invented lead time.
- **Scrapped cohort jobs:** eligible jobs that reach a declared scrap terminal outcome. These are not included in the good-job lead-time distribution.
- **Observed first-pass yield by station:** first-attempt passes divided by first-attempt outcomes observed for unique jobs whose first attempt at that station began at or after warm-up.
- **Unresolved first attempts:** eligible first attempts that started but had no observed quality outcome before the horizon. The report discloses them separately because dropping them silently would hide right-censoring.

Each seed provides its own median/P95 good-job lead time and per-station observed first-pass-yield fraction. The report aggregates these seed-level statistics across the fixed seed list. This is distinct from pooling every job across seeds into one giant sample, which would overstate independent replication. The event ledger permits independent reconstruction of the primary-seed baseline.

## First-pass yield versus final yield

A job may fail its first attempt, rework, and later pass. That is a final-good unit but not a first-pass success. The first-pass numerator counts only an observed pass at attempt number one. Rework attempts are not additional unique units in the denominator. If first-attempt outcomes are unresolved at the horizon, they appear in a separate count; the reported fraction is labeled *observed first-pass yield*, not a claim that unresolved outcomes were passes.

## Run

Use the package formatter and tests, then run the EV factory simulation binary on the versioned synthetic catalog. The report contains both the baseline event ledger and seed-level derived lifecycle summaries.

## Evidence and safety limits

The ledger is generated by the model, not obtained from a physical factory. It is useful for replay, internal consistency and synthetic benchmark construction; it does not prove a real PLC/MES stream is complete or truthful. Cross-system clock reconciliation, actual product/order genealogy, operator scan coverage, metrology, real quality-system outcomes and independent plant validation remain future work.

No supplier, factory, manufacturer, integrator, landlord or agency was contacted; no quote was requested. Product, factory, safety, legal and quality release remain false.
