## South African jurisdiction boundary

A separate South African installation profile must pin current SANS editions, amendments, statutory regulations, national adoption, CoC requirements and competent-person responsibilities. The Government Gazette notice identifying SANS 10142-1:2024 Ed 3.2 is a research pointer: https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202408/51050gen2686.pdf. The Department of Employment and Labour notice of 2025-10-22 reiterates electrical installation law and CoC requirements: https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/employment-and-labour-electrical-installation-laws-and-certificate-compliance.

This register is not complete enough to encode normative rules or claim South African conformity. Check the current authoritative SANS catalogue, licensed standard text, legislation and applicable authority before doing so.
