## Key Decisions

- NPC personhood is expressed through body, work, silence, relationships, care, learning, and institutions—not dialogue volume.
- Affect must have grounded causes and remains private unless expressed or disclosed.
- Skills change only through validated learning events.
- Institutions decompose into roles, records, procedures, resources, and power; there is no unexplained group mind.
- Generative language may choose wording but cannot create facts, permissions, relationships, quests, inventory, or history.
- Every advanced component requires ablation, fallback, privacy controls, replayable evidence, and a kill switch.
