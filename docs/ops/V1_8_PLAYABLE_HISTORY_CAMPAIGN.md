# v1.8 Playable History Campaign

## Objective

Convert v1.7 historical texture into production-bounded playable regions without reducing lore to quest dressing or weakening systemic authority.

## Added

1. Canonical-draft playable historical campaign contract.
2. Production content-packet standard.
3. Eight authored regional campaign exemplars.
4. Causal pressure, faction-clock, commitment, and campaign-state runtime.
5. Discoverable archive, ruin, evidence, and environmental-storytelling runtime.
6. Eight corporate empire campaign packets.
7. Six legendary destination production packs.
8. Eight diaspora, shadow-network, and mobile-civilization packets.
9. Cultural seasons, festivals, art, food, clothing, sport, and everyday-life bible.
10. Deterministic playable-history compiler and worldline-variation runtime.
11. Machine-readable schemas and Nine Pumps fixture.
12. Regional benchmark and staged implementation-ticket backlog.
13. v1.8 registry, roadmap, readiness, audit, and patch-reconstruction integration.

## Patch Order

```text
0001 campaign and packet contracts
0002 regional campaign anthology
0003 campaign pressure and faction runtime
0004 discoverable history runtime
0005 corporate campaign packets
0006 legendary destination production packs
0007 diaspora and shadow-network packets
0008 cultural seasons and everyday life
0009 compiler, schemas, benchmark, and tickets
0010 canon and production integration
```

## Scope Boundary

This campaign defines playable content and its proof architecture. It does not promote implementation maturity beyond `I0`.

The production target is one region with one major campaign packet, not simultaneous implementation of all legendary destinations or corporate empires.

## Governing Principle

> **The setting becomes a game when history can be worked on, argued over, repaired, inherited, and revisited through the world itself.**
