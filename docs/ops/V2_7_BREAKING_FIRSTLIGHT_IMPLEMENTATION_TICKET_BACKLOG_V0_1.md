# v2.7 Breaking Firstlight Implementation Ticket Backlog

## Delivery Principle

Build the smallest playable evidence chain first.

No team may begin broad cast generation, convoy expansion, or multiple catastrophe families until the greybox opening and one crawler route are enjoyable.

# Milestone A — Ten-Minute Greybox

- `BF-001` First-person movement and interaction baseline.
- `BF-002` Bent Feeder physical enclosure and tool contact.
- `BF-003` One material fault with two valid interventions.
- `BF-004` One injured or access-limited resident state.
- `BF-005` Bounded IRIS prompt and uncertainty response.
- `BF-006` Persistent feeder state after repair or bypass.
- `BF-007` Accessible interaction alternatives.
- `BF-008` Ten-minute telemetry and replay capture.

Gate: empty-space movement and feeder interaction are independently enjoyable.

# Required Evidence Per Ticket

Every completed ticket records:

- implementation commit;
- test or playtest evidence;
- deterministic seed where applicable;
- performance cost;
- accessibility impact;
- privacy and safety impact;
- known limitations;
- readiness grade.

# Production Maxim

> **Do not build the road to the galaxy until one damaged feeder, one generated face, one impossible warning, and one overloaded vehicle prove that the player wants to keep going.**
