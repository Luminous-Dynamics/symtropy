# v2.7 Breaking Firstlight, Unique Casts, and Mobile Continuance Campaign

## Campaign Question

Can Symtropy make players love a small, unique community in under two hours, break its stable form without invalidating that love, and turn what survives into the player's first enduring mobile home?

## Canonical Decisions

1. Firstlight's stable civic form ends during the opening.
2. Firstlight persists as people, ruins, records, ecology, claims, missing persons and possible return.
3. Independent worldlines receive unique named casts.
4. Shared multiplayer worldlines share one authoritative cast.
5. Cast roles are authored; people are generated and then persist.
6. The catastrophe is a coupled-system threshold, not a cosmetic cutscene.
7. The player cannot save everything but can materially alter continuity.
8. Missing, dead, separated and recoverable are distinct states.
9. The Continuance Crawler is a disputed vehicle and household, not universal player property.
10. Residents are persons with rights, not crew bonuses.
11. Modules obey mass, power, heat, water, labor, damage, ownership and route constraints.
12. The two-hour benchmark must pass before forty-hour expansion.

## Patch Sequence

1. Breaking contract.
2. Unique cast runtime and schema.
3. Cast role bible.
4. Two-hour experience map.
5. Catastrophe and survivor runtime.
6. Mobile-base sovereignty contract.
7. Crawler physical runtime and schema.
8. Interior and survivor-life atlas.
9. Benchmark, fixture and backlog.
10. Registry and production integration.

## First Commercial Promise

The opening should allow players to say:

- “These were my people.”
- “I know what I chose.”
- “I do not know what happened to everyone.”
- “This damaged vehicle contains what I carried forward.”
- “I want to see where the road leads.”

## Evidence Boundary

This campaign remains documentation evidence only.

No generated resident, catastrophe state, mobile-base system, or player-love claim is implemented by the existence of these files.

## Campaign Maxim

> **The game begins when a unique home becomes history and the player discovers that survival means choosing what kind of world can still be carried forward.**
