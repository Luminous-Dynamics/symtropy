# v1.7 Historical Texture Campaign

## Objective

Give Symtropy memorable names, places, institutions, songs, foods, scandals, diasporas, ruins, and disputed histories without weakening causality or turning lore into static exposition.

## Added

1. Megacorporate empire and post-corporate successor-state bible.
2. Eight named corporate civilization exemplars.
3. Fourteen great conflicts, catastrophes, and historical scars.
4. Sixteen legendary cities, habitats, and world regions.
5. Twelve diasporas, stateless peoples, and mobile civilizations.
6. Eighteen historical figures with contested legacies and erased networks.
7. Twelve shadow civilizations and informal institutions.
8. Artistic movements, food, clothing, media, and material culture.
9. Competing historiographic schools and propaganda grammars.
10. Historical timeline and relationship map.
11. Causal lore provenance and generation runtime.
12. Historical texture and world-coherence benchmark.

## Scope Boundary

This campaign expands historical texture, not the current implementation claim.

The first proof remains one bounded region where several systems intersect visibly. No number of lore entries substitutes for playable causal evidence.
