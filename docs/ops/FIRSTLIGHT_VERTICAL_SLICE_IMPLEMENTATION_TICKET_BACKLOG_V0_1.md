# Explicit Deferrals

Before M7, do not prioritize:

- full planetary travel;
- playable Atlas routes;
- broad alien civilization content;
- large dynasty roster;
- dozens of professions;
- universal destruction;
- procedural city generation;
- extensive weapon catalogue;
- final monetization systems.

## Backlog Maxim

> **Build the first remembered evening before the first hundred worlds.**
