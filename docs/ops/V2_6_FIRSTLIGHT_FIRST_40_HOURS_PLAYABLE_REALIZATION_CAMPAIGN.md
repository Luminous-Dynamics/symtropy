# v2.6 Firstlight First 40 Hours Playable Realization Campaign

## Objective

Concentrate Symtropy's existing design into one opening region players can love for ten to forty hours.

# Patch Series

1. player love and retention contract;
2. first ten and forty hour experience map;
3. worldspace, landmarks and traversal;
4. core action, danger, rescue and destruction;
5. three profession mastery proof;
6. IRIS, companions and emotional hooks;
7. campaign and ordinary-life anthology;
8. pacing, discovery, return and accessibility;
9. benchmark, schemas and implementation backlog;
10. production integration, registry, roadmap, readiness and audit.

# Product Target

The target is one high-density Firstlight region containing:

- six flagship relationships;
- at least twenty-four recurring residents;
- twelve anchor landmarks;
- three professions;
- four action scenarios;
- one cultural event;
- one hearing;
- one continuity case;
- one absence and changed return;
- enough horizon to suggest Earth, orbit, planets, seed ships and Atlas routes without building them first.

# Governing Rule

> **Players should not need forty hours before Symtropy becomes good. The first ten minutes, first evening, first danger and first learned skill must already justify the next hour.**

# Evidence

v2.6 provides implementation-ready design and machine-readable fixtures.

It does not prove:

- fun;
- emotional attachment;
- production performance;
- accessible interaction;
- deterministic simulation;
- player recall;
- ethical retention.

Those require the benchmark and real participants.
