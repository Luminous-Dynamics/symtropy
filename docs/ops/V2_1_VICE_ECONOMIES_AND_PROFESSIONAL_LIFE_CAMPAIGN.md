# v2.1 Vice Economies and Professional Life Campaign

## Campaign Thesis

> **Prove one city by proving the work that keeps it alive.**

v2.1 combines two previously separate directions:

- a functional player-built pleasure city;
- deep profession gameplay.

The pleasure district provides a bounded systemic test. Profession depth prevents the city from becoming a theme park, abstract economy, or sequence of crisis popups.

# Ordered Patch Groups

1. vice-economy and pleasure-city canon;
2. twenty-four-hour district metabolism;
3. gambling, credit, and game integrity;
4. adult-venue worker sovereignty and consent;
5. nightlife harm reduction and emergency response;
6. regulation, informal markets, corruption, and organized crime;
7. profession mastery and shift/project/career runtime;
8. twelve profession packets and implementation backlog;
9. seven-day benchmark and machine-readable fixtures;
10. canon, roadmap, readiness, audits, and packaging.

# Authority Changes

- player-built pleasure cities are explicitly supported;
- there is no universal `sin` or `vice` meter;
- the profession mastery contract becomes canonical authority over the earlier profession-loop document;
- adult-work participation remains controlled by adulthood, consent, worker sovereignty, and privacy;
- IRIS remains advisory and private, not morality police;
- design maturity does not imply implementation.

# Best Implementation Order

1. district utilities, schedules, staffing, transport, sanitation, and housing;
2. sanitation, nightlife medicine, and maintenance professions;
3. gambling custody and public-trust professions;
4. cultural and hospitality professions;
5. adult venue only after all safeguards;
6. informal markets and corruption;
7. full benchmark;
8. profession generalization to the wider game.

# What v2.1 Does Not Claim

- that explicit adult content is implemented;
- that every profession in Symtropy is fully designed;
- that a pleasure city is required for every campaign;
- that harm is more realistic than joy;
- that regulation or prohibition has one correct outcome;
- that documentation proves fun or feasibility.

# Central Production Risk

The largest risk is not controversy. It is turning deep systems into repetitive chores.

Every preparation, cleanup, and administrative interaction must earn its place through judgment, scarcity, social meaning, consequence, or relief.

# Promotion Criterion

The campaign succeeds when blind players can identify distinct professions by how they perceive and act, while the district remains financially, materially, socially, and politically causal across a full week and long absence.
