# v2.4 Atlas Metric Engineering, FTL Chronology, and Galactic Reach Campaign

## Campaign Decision

Symtropy permits FTL travel.

It does so through future-directed Atlas metric infrastructure rather than unrestricted ship warp.

This preserves:

- player access to many worlds;
- seed ships;
- relativistic time;
- local sovereignty;
- historical divergence;
- multiplayer causality;
- meaningful logistics;
- route labor and politics;
- alien difference.

# Ten-Patch Sequence

1. Atlas FTL and causality contract.
2. Four-time chronology and causal graph runtime.
3. Metric trim, sail, anchor, corridor, and bridge runtime.
4. Seed ship and player continuity contract.
5. Multiplayer epoch migration and asynchronous regions.
6. Route cultures and ordinary interstellar life.
7. Alien metric traditions and causal contact.
8. Warfare, rescue, quarantine, failure, and scars.
9. First reconnection benchmark, fixtures, and backlog.
10. Canon, dependency, roadmap, readiness, registry, and release integration.

# Core Decisions

## FTL Is Allowed

Players can travel personally to connected worlds within playable time.

## First Arrival Still Requires Distance

A star without compatible receiving infrastructure requires a physical crossing or independently developed destination route.

## Atlas Time Is Fictional but Explicit

All FTL must remain future-directed in one declared worldline.

## Four Times Remain Distinct

Atlas, civic, proper, and knowledge time may disagree without contradiction.

## Gate Is a Civic Complex

The visible endpoint is supported by clocks, power, cooling, labor, traffic, quarantine, housing, rescue, and law.

## Seed Descendants Are Sovereign

They may refuse or constrain reconnection.

## Multiplayer Separation Is Real

One player's voyage does not force all players to skip years and does not leave a duplicate behind.

## Alien Routes Are Not Reskins

Compatibility may remain partial, political, ecological, or impossible.

# Representative Proof

The first proof is:

```text
Echo Two
→ Muni Seventeen seed voyage
→ Far Station founding
→ local Atlas debate
→ staged pairing
→ positive-latency first crossing
→ chronology attack rejection
→ route emergency
→ Atlas Scar
→ five-year aftermath
```

# Evidence Boundary

This campaign delivers documents, schemas, fixtures, checksums, and exact patch reconstruction.

It does not deliver runtime implementation.

All capabilities remain D3 / I0.

# Production Maxim

> **The galaxy becomes playable when travel is possible. It becomes Symtropy when every crossing has a clock, a history, a workforce, a receiving society, and a cost that remains after arrival.**
