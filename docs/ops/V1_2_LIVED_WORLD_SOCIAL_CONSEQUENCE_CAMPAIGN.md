# v1.2 Lived-World Social Consequence Campaign

## Goal

Make Symtropy's social world carry consequences beyond a single conversation or crisis scene.

The campaign specifies how information spreads, bodies change, care is organized, harm is investigated, adult relationships preserve consent, households migrate, belief becomes lived practice, and corrections or repairs remain incomplete in different people.

## Patch Groups

1. information ecology, rumor, media, and reputation contract;
2. social-signal and public-opinion runtime;
3. health, trauma, recovery, and care contract;
4. body-health and recovery runtime;
5. justice, harm, accountability, and repair contract;
6. adult relationship, intimacy, romance, and boundary contract;
7. migration, diaspora, belonging, and integration contract;
8. belief, ritual, religion, and meaning contract;
9. integrated lived-world social-consequence benchmark;
10. canon, roadmap, readiness, registry, manifest, and audit integration.

## Representative Proof

One Firstlight district runs through ordinary life, arrival, route failure, injury, rumor, media publication, justice inquiry, relationship boundary, mourning, correction, fourteen days of player absence, save/load, and worldline fork.

## Product Claim Boundary

This campaign permits the claim that Symtropy has a bounded design and validation architecture for lived social consequences.

It does not permit claims that the systems are implemented, psychologically accurate, medically authoritative, legally prescriptive, or capable of human-equivalent social understanding.

## Kill Discipline

Remove or simplify any component that fails to improve player comprehension, character continuity, or meaningful consequence relative to deterministic baseline—or that causes critical privacy, consent, authority, performance, or authoring regressions.
