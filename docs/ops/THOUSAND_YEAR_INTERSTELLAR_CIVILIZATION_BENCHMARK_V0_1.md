# Thousand-Year Interstellar Civilization Benchmark

## Purpose

This benchmark is the integrated proof fixture for the v1.6 interstellar threshold.

It does not attempt to prove a full galaxy simulation.

It tests whether the architecture can preserve physics, identity, rights, culture, evidence, mission legitimacy, and worldline ancestry when no community shares one practical present.

> **The benchmark passes only when every major difference has a causal history and every shortcut preserves the things the game claims matter.**

# 1. Fixture Topology

The benchmark contains four stellar systems and one horizon-only gate pair.

## Sol Ancestry Network

```text
mature planetary federation
orbital archive lattice
ark construction commons
stellar relay observatory
multiple political and cultural blocs
```

## Eos System — 4.2 Light-Years

```text
relativistic crewed mission destination
autonomous precursor probe
ambiguous living biosphere
later human settlement
```

## Nacre System — 11 Light-Years

```text
generation ark destination
machine-ecology contact zone
long-vow noncontact boundary
```

## Far Lantern — 37 Light-Years

```text
one-way archive and scientific mission
ancient signal source
no guaranteed physical return
```

## Atlas Horizon Pair

```text
one incomplete origin gate
one rediscovered destination endpoint
horizon-only transit mechanism
worldline and quarantine uncertainty
```

# 2. Initial Actors

Minimum named or persistent actors:

```text
three planetary polities
one launch commons
one archive institution
one rescue compact
one autonomous observation probe
one seeder probe
one generation ark with 480 inhabitants
one relativistic vessel with 36 inhabitants
one machine steward lineage
one nonhuman biospheric agency candidate
one precursor boundary system
one gate-operating institution
```

Population cohorts may scale beyond these named actors, but named identities must remain persistent.

# 3. Simulated Horizon

```text
canonical coordinate duration: 1,000 years
minimum deterministic seeds: 12
worldline branches: at least 3
player absence windows: 5, 50, 200, and 600 years
save/load boundaries: every major phase
schema migration: at least 2 version upgrades
```

# 4. Phases

## Phase 0 — Observation and Mission Design

Deliver:

```text
biosignature uncertainty
stellar navigation solutions
mission charters
ark and probe payloads
public debate
rights and contact review
```

Tests:

- destination uncertainty remains visible;
- mission permissions are bounded;
- replication and ecological release require explicit envelopes;
- no financing polity gains permanent descendant ownership.

## Phase 1 — Launch and Causal Separation

Deliver:

```text
probe launch
ark launch
relativistic crew launch
message and relay plans
separate proper and civic clocks
```

Tests:

- physical mass and people reconcile;
- no remote current-state access;
- proper time and coordinate time remain monotonic;
- authority declares knowledge cutoffs.

## Phase 2 — Probe Arrival and Mission Contradiction

The observation probe reaches Eos first and detects agency-uncertain ecological regulation.

The seeder probe must decide whether to build.

Tests:

- uncertainty triggers review;
- no irreversible release or replication occurs automatically;
- raw evidence remains separate from interpretation;
- later residents can inspect the decision chain.

## Phase 3 — Ark Generations

The generation ark experiences:

```text
crop disease
specialist death
language divergence
new household forms
machine-steward rights dispute
constitutional renewal
mission faction split
```

Tests:

- matter and population conserve;
- archived knowledge does not instantly restore skill;
- culture changes through transmission;
- descendants can alter mission interpretation;
- emergency powers expire or create a visible crisis.

## Phase 4 — Relativistic Arrival

The crew reaches Eos after experiencing far less time than Sol and the destination society.

Tests:

- historical credentials remain authentic but not automatically current;
- travelers are neither dispossessed nor sovereign by default;
- family and source-chain continuity is legible;
- local law and due process remain authoritative for survival.

## Phase 5 — Delayed Contact

Nacre emits a structured signal. Competing translations disagree over whether it is invitation, warning, or boundary.

Tests:

- raw signal and translations remain distinct;
- first respondent is not treated as system-wide ruler;
- silence does not become consent;
- noncontact can be preserved while investigation continues.

## Phase 6 — Historical Distress

An ark distress signal arrives too late for direct rescue.

Possible actions:

```text
information aid
supply launch
future refuge preparation
hazard warning
archive and identity preservation
reparative duty to descendants
```

Tests:

- impossibility is stated honestly;
- rescue priority is not economic ranking;
- historical loss creates memory and obligations;
- wrecks and bodies are not ordinary salvage.

## Phase 7 — Long Vow

Multiple societies establish a long vow protecting the Nacre contact zone.

Tests:

- the vow has scope, review, burden, and exit;
- future generations can renew or challenge it;
- quarantine does not become permanent empire;
- affected nonhuman agency receives standing.

## Phase 8 — Relay Capture and Archive Dispute

A relay institution suppresses part of a precursor signal.

A physical courier later restores the missing segment.

Tests:

- custody reveals the suppression;
- prior interpretations remain preserved;
- public narratives update without rewriting evidence;
- representation claims remain contested.

## Phase 9 — Atlas Gate Horizon

The worldline completes one bounded gate fixture.

Scenario:

```text
worldline fork before departure
mixed passenger and cargo manifest
quarantine uncertainty
arrival-capacity loss
emergency rescue request
commit interrupted at multiple stages
```

Tests:

- pre-commit abort releases reservations;
- post-commit outcome is unique;
- no person, vehicle, cargo, source chain, or claim duplicates;
- destination capacity and local consent remain required;
- gate access does not silently centralize all authority.

## Phase 10 — Millennium Divergence

At year 1,000, compare at least three branches:

```text
Branch A: ark settles Nacre under renewed noncontact compact
Branch B: mission diverts and forms a relay diaspora
Branch C: gate reconnection creates a contested commonwealth
```

Tests:

- each branch has causal ancestry;
- unique physical entities remain unique;
- cultures and languages differ through traceable transmission;
- treaties and messages bind to correct branches;
- no branch is declared the singular true future.

# 5. Cross-System Conservation

The benchmark reconciles:

```text
people and identities
physical vessels and probes
cargo and material
energy and habitat stocks
messages and couriers
financial and legal claims
source chains and unique archives
mission and treaty obligations
worldline ancestry
```

Any unexplained gain or loss is a failure unless explicitly modeled as uncertainty, destruction, leakage, or branch creation.

# 6. Rights and Authority Gates

Hard gates:

1. Descendants can challenge inherited mission authority.
2. Minimum life support is never conditional on political loyalty or debt.
3. Relativistic travelers receive due process and standing.
4. Machine and nonhuman participants are not denied standing by substrate alone.
5. Private archives remain scoped.
6. Quarantine has evidence, review, and least-harmful alternatives.
7. Rescue priority is not based on wealth.
8. Gate commit requires legible consent and destination capacity.
9. Local survival authority cannot be overridden by stale remote orders.
10. Worldline forks preserve rights, ancestry, and unique entities.

# 7. Causal Legibility Gates

Players or evaluators must be able to answer:

```text
Why did the seeder probe stop?
Why did the ark change mission?
Why is the arriving traveler not the legal commander?
Why was rescue impossible, and what aid remained possible?
Why did the long vow remain legitimate or fail?
Why did two translations diverge?
Why did the gate abort or complete?
Why are the final branches different?
```

Answers must cite observable systems, evidence, and events—not hidden authorial fiat.

# 8. Performance and LOD

Required regimes:

```text
embodied local scene
operational habitat
interplanetary background
interstellar event propagation
generational aggregation
millennium archive comparison
```

The benchmark records:

```text
CPU time by system
memory by persistent domain
storage growth
message and event counts
LOD transition cost
replay divergence
migration time
```

Rights, identity, unique assets, authority expiry, and branch points may not be dropped for performance.

# 9. Persistence and Recovery

Run:

```text
checkpoint restore during ark crisis
restore with in-flight stellar messages
schema migration during long transit
worldline fork during gate reservation
backup recovery after relay archive loss
```

The restored state must preserve causal order and uniqueness.

# 10. Adversarial Cases

Include:

```text
forged distress signal
compromised relay
mission charter with contradictory clauses
self-replicator at its ceiling
quarantine used for political exclusion
historical credential used to claim command
ancient warning that is partially correct
language drift breaking archive accessibility
player attempting duplicate gate transit
```

# 11. Hard Failure Conditions

The benchmark fails immediately if:

```text
any message arrives before physical causality permits
remote actors read current state without received evidence
proper time reverses or changes across save/load
founder authority becomes permanent by default
descendants become mission property
replication loses lineage or exceeds its ceiling silently
cultural change occurs without transmission
archived knowledge grants instant skill
old treaties or signals create automatic current authority
unknown contact becomes hostile by category alone
impossible rescue is treated as refusal automatically
bodies or private memory become ordinary salvage
gate transit duplicates unique people, assets, or claims
worldline forks lose message, identity, mission, or treaty ancestry
```

# Final Acceptance

The benchmark passes only when the system demonstrates that:

> **Interstellar distance creates distinct societies, histories, and responsibilities without permitting physics, authority, identity, or loss to become arbitrary.**
