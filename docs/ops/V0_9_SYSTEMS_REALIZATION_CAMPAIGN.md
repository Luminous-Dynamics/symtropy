# Symtropy v0.9 Systems Realization Campaign

## Purpose

v0.9 closes the largest remaining gap between the design corpus and a buildable, scalable game: several central fantasies had strong vision documents but no authoritative runtime, content, LOD, migration, or proof contracts.

The campaign adds no mandatory galaxy-scale content to Seedworks. It defines bounded implementations and representative proofs.

## Patch 1 — Living Worlds

Adds:

```text
canonical ecology and terraforming meaning
habitat fields and functional guilds
visible species cohorts
flows and conservation
regime shifts and hysteresis
introductions, quarantine, and alien agency evidence
planetary-program architecture with strict Seedworks deferral
```

## Patch 2 — Built Worlds

Adds:

```text
construction and repair grammar
structural graph and load cases
material provenance and conservation
foundations, utilities, commissioning, and degradation
progressive failure and bounded debris
multiplayer construction authority
```

## Patch 3 — Mobility and Expeditions

Adds:

```text
shared vehicle operations across ground, water, air, rail, and space
route and expedition loops
crew stations and solo automation
cargo, passengers, energy, thermal, and life support
vehicle damage, rescue, towing, and recovery
orbital-operation architecture
```

## Patch 4 — First Contact and Xenotechnics

Adds:

```text
contact phases
competing hypotheses
controlled experiments
scoped signal correspondences
boundaries and category-violence risk
bidirectional translation
xenotechnology dependencies and safety
```

## Patch 5 — Procedural and Content Realization

Adds:

## Explicit Non-Goals

v0.9 does not commit Seedworks to:

```text
planetary terraforming
freeform city-scale destruction
full flight and orbital game
complete alien language
unbounded procedural planets
runtime-generated commercial music
large persistent crowds
```

These systems must first pass the bounded proof tracks.

## Recommended Proof Order

1. Causal explanation envelope and attention router.
2. Structural fixture with material conservation and utility commissioning.
3. One expedition vehicle with cargo, route, degraded failure, and recovery.
4. One habitat patch with flow, intervention, delay, and LOD transition.
5. One ambiguous responsive contact with competing hypotheses.
6. One deterministic generated site with full provenance and validators.
7. One combined ordinary-life/storm benchmark with semantic audio.

## Completion Evidence

The campaign is complete when:

- ordered patches apply cleanly to v0.8;
- the reconstructed tree matches v0.9;
- canonical ownership and supersession are explicit;
- active Markdown links resolve;
- active canonical and implementation documents have required metadata;
- no active duplicate title creates ambiguous authority;
- the registry, manifest, and audits are regenerated.

## Final Rule

```text
v0.9 does not make Symtropy larger.
It makes the existing promise harder to misunderstand and easier to build.
```
