# v2.5 Worldline Characters, Interstellar Houses, Succession, and Political Memory Campaign

## Campaign Decision

A player may control many characters.

Those characters may coexist in one worldline or live in separately preserved branches. They retain distinct bodies, knowledge, relationships, property, offices, source chains, and proper time.

Symtropy may support a vast interstellar political epic. It does so through infrastructure, succession, family, labor, memory, and distance rather than a universal betrayal generator.

# Ten-Patch Sequence

1. Multi-character and perspective-authority contract.
2. Worldline roster, handoff, and continuity runtime.
3. Character knowledge and anti-metagaming runtime.
4. Houses, lineages, guilds, and civic succession contract.
5. Inheritance, office, property, reconstitution, and claims runtime.
6. Seed-ship generations and mission-politics atlas.
7. Atlas route houses, chokepoints, diplomacy, and blockade atlas.
8. Marriage, adoption, apprenticeship, wards, and hostage guardrails.
9. Political anthology, three-generation benchmark, schemas, and backlog.
10. Canon, dependency, roadmap, readiness, registry, and release integration.

# Core Decisions

## Many Characters Are Allowed

The account may contain many playable lives.

## Same Worldline Is Normal

Characters need not be separated into alternate timelines. Strong political stories often require coexistence.

## Branches Remain Separate

Divergent histories cannot exchange people, assets, evidence, or authority.

## The Account Is Not a God

It owns access and settings, not one universal in-world identity.

## Inactive Characters Live

They work, age, change relationships, and make bounded autonomous choices.

## Knowledge Is Character-Bound

The player can remember more than the active character. Actions still require valid evidence.

## Houses Are Plural

Biological, adoptive, seed, machine, professional, corporate, civic, religious, alien, and mixed lineages are valid.

## Reconstitution Does Not Rewind Succession

A returned person can be real while the successor remains legitimate.

## Relationships Are Not Assets

Marriage, adoption, apprenticeship, and wardship retain consent, care, and exit.

## Peace Is Playable

Orderly succession and competent administration receive full systemic depth.

# Evidence Boundary

This campaign delivers:

- canonical and technical documents;
- lore atlases;
- campaign anthology;
- JSON schemas and fixtures;
- implementation backlog;
- integration audit;
- exact patch reconstruction.

It does not deliver runtime implementation.

All new capabilities remain **D3 / I0**.

# Production Maxim

> **Let the player inhabit history through many lives. Never let the account erase the boundaries that make those lives matter.**
