# Symtropy Founding Triangle Start Flow

## Version 0.1 — Origin, Charter, Worldline

## Purpose

This document turns the three start-layer systems into a playable opening flow:

```text id="56uqp2"
Origin: Where did you come from?
Charter: What kind of society are you trying to build?
Worldline: What kind of history wounded the world?
```

Together, these form the **Founding Triangle**.

The Founding Triangle should make the player feel, from the beginning, that Symtropy is not merely about surviving a world.

It is about testing futures.

## Core Thesis

The player does not begin as a blank hero.

The player begins as someone carrying history.

```text id="owgrrn"
Your Origin defines what you recognize.
Your Charter defines what you believe repair is for.
Your Worldline defines what wounded the world around you.
```

## Design Goal

The start flow should answer three emotional questions:

```text id="bf85p5"
Where did you learn what civilization means?
What kind of society do you want to help build?
What kind of broken future are you entering?
```

## Start Flow Overview

Recommended eventual start flow:

```text id="bkeyka"
1. Choose Worldline
2. Choose Origin
3. Choose or inherit Settlement Charter
4. Receive Field Deck
5. Enter Firstlight Basin
6. Encounter Water Crisis
7. Investigate Old Waterworks
8. See Origin + Charter + Worldline reflected in the same pump
```

For v0.1, this can be hardcoded.

Do not build a full character creator yet.

First, make the Old Waterworks respond to three mocked origins and one charter.


## Recommended Default Campaign

The default first campaign should be:

```text id="nprwm5"
Worldline: Seed Age
Settlement Charter: Firstlight Public Repair Charter
Location: Firstlight Basin
Opening Site: Old Waterworks
Year: 2168
```

The player can eventually choose origin.

For the first playable slice, support three origins:

```text id="2pfx2a"
Basin-Born Technician
Archive Apprentice
Corporate Utility Defector
```

These three are enough to prove the Founding Triangle works.

## Default Charter: Firstlight Public Repair Charter

The opening settlement should begin with a charter that embodies the core Symtropy thesis.

```text id="s1gdj0"
1. Water is a public trust.
2. Survival infrastructure must remain auditable.
3. Emergency powers expire unless witnessed and renewed.
4. No machine may deny public override without a recorded reason.
5. No person may be bound to a settlement by debt alone.
6. Repair knowledge must be taught across generations.
7. Archive Witness is required for dead-authority overrides.
8. Ecological restoration is part of infrastructure maintenance.
9. Outsiders may request refuge, but resource strain must be publicly reviewed.
10. Any charter article may be challenged through Confluence hearing.
```

Opening conflict:

```text id="bw8t5z"
The Old Waterworks is public survival infrastructure,
but the pump remains locked under expired emergency authority.
```

This immediately tests the charter.

## First Hour Structure

## Scene 1 — Arrival or Wake

The player enters Firstlight Basin.

Possible framing:

```text id="mvuiey"
You arrive during water rationing.
The settlement has survived, but not comfortably.
The Field Deck is issued, repaired, inherited, stolen, or reactivated depending on origin.
```

The player sees:

* water queue
* ration marks
* public notice board
* broken pipe segment
* faction argument
* children carrying containers
* old floodline or drought marker
* inactive pump route sign

Goal:

```text id="78k6bp"
Make water political before making it mechanical.
```

## Scene 2 — Origin Signal

The same opening situation should read differently depending on origin.

### Basin-Born Technician

```text id="c64h73"
You recognize worker repair marks on old utility panels.
An NPC calls you by family or guild association.
The basin feels personal.
```

### Archive Apprentice

```text id="bd0thi"
You notice that public notices reference an expired authority chain.
The ration order is legally unstable.
The crisis smells like a record problem.
```

### Corporate Utility Defector

```text id="mwu7dc"
You recognize lock architecture from private utility firmware.
The public infrastructure has been touched by contract logic.
Someone may have privatized part of the system.
```

Design goal:

```text id="8dr6vw"
The player should understand that origin changes perception, not just stats.
```

## Scene 3 — Charter Conflict Introduced

NPCs argue about the charter.

Example:

```text id="7u6lu9"
Resident:
  "Article One says water is public. So why are we still rationing?"

Security Officer:
  "Article Three says emergency powers can be renewed."

Archivist:
  "Renewed, yes. Not inherited from the dead."

Engineer:
  "None of this matters if the pump burns out while we debate."
```

The Field Deck can show:

```text id="yn9rjh"
CIVIC MODE:
Relevant Charter Articles:
1. Water is a public trust.
3. Emergency powers expire unless witnessed and renewed.
7. Archive Witness required for dead-authority overrides.

Conflict:
Old Waterworks authority status unresolved.
```

Goal:

```text id="9dmdt4"
Make the charter feel like gameplay, not lore.
```

## Scene 4 — First Walk to Old Waterworks

The player travels to the Old Waterworks.

Environmental storytelling:

```text id="i5syy7"
faded drought ration signs
painted floodlines
worker initials
company-logo scrape marks
emergency seal symbols
Null signal flicker
old public works badge
children’s chalk marks
abandoned hand pump
```

The worldline modifies visual tone.

### Seed Age

```text id="nnvpxt"
wounded but hopeful, repair flags, public notices, gardens near broken infrastructure
```

### Flood Noir

```text id="23qqzr"
rain, pumps, lower-district resentment, waterline stains, insurance markers
```

### Ghost Industrial

```text id="qo8ysx"
heavy machinery, toxic runoff, automated warning systems, labor murals
```

### Corporate Utility Dystopia

```text id="g4fzxa"
clean signage, subscription meters, private firmware seals, guarded access points
```

## Scene 5 — Field Deck Activation

The Field Deck becomes the main interpretive tool.

Initial modes:

```text id="6n4d7i"
SCAN
DIAG
ARCHIVE
CIVIC
NULL
```

The same pump reads differently in each mode.

```text id="dm488v"
SCAN:
Pump casing cracked. Valve corrosion severe.

DIAG:
PUMP_1 locked. Tank level 12%.

ARCHIVE:
Built 2048. Modified under Emergency Water Act 2087.
Authority chain failed approximately 2113.

CIVIC:
Public override blocked. Charter conflict detected.

NULL:
Repeated lock reinforcement detected.
```

Goal:

```text id="fkmmjs"
The Field Deck does not show truth.
It shows layers of responsibility.
```

## Scene 6 — Founding Triangle Reflected in the Pump

The Old Waterworks should respond to Origin, Charter, and Worldline.

## Origin Reflection

### Basin-Born Technician

```text id="77ihcl"
FIELD DECK NOTE:
Worker repair marks match local family-line maintenance symbols.

NPC Memory:
"My grandmother said the pump sounded like thunder when it still worked."
```

### Archive Apprentice

```text id="upg8n9"
FIELD DECK NOTE:
Authority chain incomplete. Witness protocol recommended before override.

NPC Memory:
"The law did expire. The proof is damaged. That means we witness carefully, not slowly."
```

### Corporate Utility Defector

```text id="povhmq"
FIELD DECK NOTE:
Lock architecture resembles private utility firmware despite public-works markings.

NPC Memory:
"This is how they did it. Keep the public badge, replace the heart with contract code."
```

## Charter Reflection

If settlement charter is Firstlight Public Repair Charter:

```text id="tgts8l"
Conflict:
Manual bypass restores water faster but violates Article 7.

Available paths:
- Archive Witness Override
- Public Assembly Vote
- Manual Illegal Bypass
- Machine Testimony Petition
```

## Worldline Reflection

### Seed Age

```text id="ff12g3"
The pump is a test of repair legitimacy.
```

### Flood Noir

```text id="05bcx9"
The pump may reveal who kept upper districts dry.
```

### Ghost Industrial

```text id="dbzlxl"
The pump is part of a dead labor system still enforcing old rules.
```

### Corporate Utility Dystopia

```text id="uuxo39"
The pump is public on paper and private in firmware.
```

Goal:

```text id="d2cfy6"
The same room becomes different depending on the Founding Triangle.
```

## Scene 7 — First Repair Choice

The player faces the first meaningful repair decision.

## Path A — Archive Witness Override

```text id="edz1ag"
Slowest.
Most legitimate.
Requires damaged record review.
May delay water restoration.
Reduces long-term legitimacy debt.
```

Consequences:

```text id="0ae1ly"
Archive faction trust increases.
Security faction may complain.
Residents may suffer short-term frustration.
Null drift reduced if successful.
```

## Path B — Manual Illegal Bypass

```text id="81w1t4"
Fastest.
Technically effective.
Politically dangerous.
May damage records or public trust.
```

Consequences:

```text id="vzhcgc"
Water returns quickly.
Legitimacy debt increases.
Archive trust decreases.
Open Valve-style factions approve.
Security may use precedent later.
```

## Path C — Machine Testimony Petition

```text id="5bwdzq"
Medium speed.
Requires listening to pump diagnostics.
May reveal Null contamination.
Useful for Machine Steward or technical origins.
```

Consequences:

```text id="93y9o6"
Machine Steward trust increases.
Human-only factions become suspicious.
Null risk may be identified early.
```

## Path D — Public Assembly Vote

```text id="smg8fb"
Socially legitimate if community trust is high.
Slow and vulnerable to faction conflict.
Can create strong public ownership.
```

Consequences:

```text id="7n31vl"
Mutualist trust increases.
Industrial factions may see paralysis.
Outcome depends on NPC memories and evidence shown.
```

## Scene 8 — Consequence and Chronicle

The outcome becomes history.

Example Archive Witness success:

```text id="m0wdfu"
CHRONICLE:
2168 — The Old Waterworks were restored under Archive Witness after the dead authority chain was overturned.
Water returned with public legitimacy.
```

Example illegal bypass:

```text id="voheqd"
CHRONICLE:
2168 — The Old Waterworks were restored through unwitnessed manual bypass.
Water returned quickly.
Legitimacy debt increased.
```

Example partial repair:

```text id="01ap5w"
CHRONICLE:
2168 — The Old Waterworks resumed partial flow.
The pump remained under disputed authority.
The settlement entered ration review.
```

Goal:

```text id="qynxz0"
Player action becomes future history.
```

## Implementation Guardrails

```text id="dpxqac"

```

## Success Criteria

The Founding Triangle works when:

```text id="dn3n6n"
The same pump feels different depending on player origin.
The charter changes what repair means.
The worldline changes the tone of the site.
NPCs disagree for understandable historical reasons.
The player sees that technical success and legitimate repair are not the same.
The Chronicle records the chosen outcome.
```

## Final Principle

The start of Symtropy should not ask only:

```text id="m6ujfb"
What are you good at?
```

It should ask:

```text id="1xzcxi"
What history do you carry?
What society are you willing to test?
What future wounded this world?
```

The first pump teaches the whole game.

```text id="yaxvja"
Symtropy begins when the player learns that repairing a machine means entering history.
```
