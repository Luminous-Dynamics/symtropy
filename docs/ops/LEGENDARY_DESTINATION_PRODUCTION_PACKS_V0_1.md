# Legendary Destination Production Packs

## Purpose

A legendary place must be recognizable through movement, sound, work, law, and ordinary life before a title card names it.

This document scopes six destinations as production packs suitable for vertical-slice planning. It does not commit all six to the first representative build.

## Shared Destination Budget

A full destination target contains:

```text
4–6 major districts
8–12 named inhabitants in active simulation
20–40 recurring ambient roles
3 primary infrastructure systems
2 ordinary-life loops
1 major campaign packet
3 minor historical discoveries
1 local cultural season
5 revisit state families
```

The minimum viable proof uses two districts, four named inhabitants, one infrastructure loop, one ordinary-life loop, and one bounded campaign pressure.

## Blind Distinctiveness Test

Reviewers receive silent or unlabeled captures showing:

- approach silhouette;
- one transit sequence;
- one work activity;
- one domestic public space;
- one crisis response;
- one night acoustic sample.

A destination passes when reviewers reliably distinguish it and can describe what keeps people alive there without reading lore text.

## Cross-Destination Reuse

Reuse may include:

- structural kits;
- tools;
- animation families;
- clothing layers;
- generic service devices;
- crowd behaviors.

Reuse must be transformed through local materials, maintenance history, climate, gravity, law, and culture. Identical modular assets may not produce interchangeable places.
