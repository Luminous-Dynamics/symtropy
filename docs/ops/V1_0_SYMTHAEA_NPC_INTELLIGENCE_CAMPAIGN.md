# v1.0 Symthaea NPC Intelligence Campaign

## Goal

Convert the idea of advanced Symthaea-powered citizens into a bounded, testable, privacy-preserving production program.

## Patch Groups

```text
0001 bounded integration contract
0002 cognition bridge architecture
0003 social cognition and theory of mind
0004 memory consolidation and worldline continuity
0005 cognitive rights, privacy, and player boundaries
0006 four-NPC benchmark household
0007 ablation, evaluation, and playtest program
0008 grounded NPC authoring standard
0009 canon, roadmap, readiness, registry, and audit integration
```

## Product Claim Boundary

v1.0 permits the claim:

```text
Symtropy has a concrete program for testing bounded Symthaea cognition in selected NPCs.
```

It does not permit claims of conscious NPCs, human-level AGI citizens, or proven gameplay improvement.

## Primary Proof

The representative proof is four inhabitants sharing one difficult settlement week.

Success is measured by grounded action, coherent memory, different interpretations, relationship-specific behavior, off-screen projects, calibrated belief revision, privacy, performance, and causal ablation.

## Kill Discipline

Any component may be removed if it fails to provide measurable player value.

The deterministic baseline remains a first-class architecture.
