# v1.1 Embodied Social Intelligence Campaign

## Goal

Move the NPC program beyond cognition-in-a-box and prove that selected inhabitants can live through bodies, households, education, institutions, speech, silence, care, work, aging, loss, and player absence.

## Patch Groups

1. NPC embodiment, affect, and nonverbal expression contract;
2. embodied performance and voice runtime;
3. life course, households, kinship, care, and education contract;
4. learning, teaching, apprenticeship, and skill transmission runtime;
5. institutional collective cognition and public reason runtime;
6. grounded dialogue, voice, and generative safety runtime;
7. longitudinal social-ecology benchmark;
8. observability, evidence, and failure-triage standard;
9. canon, roadmap, readiness, registry, and audit integration.

## Representative Proof

One twelve-person district continues across ordinary days, a route and care crisis, rumor, public decision, apprenticeship, festival, partial reconstitution, player absence, and worldline fork.

## Product Claim Boundary

The campaign permits the claim that Symtropy has a testable architecture for embodied, longitudinal, socially grounded NPCs with deterministic fallback.

It does not permit claims of consciousness, AGI citizens, human equivalence, mental-health capability, or proven implementation.

## Kill Discipline

Any optional component is removed when it fails to beat the deterministic baseline on preregistered player value, grounding, privacy, cost, authoring burden, or comprehensibility.
