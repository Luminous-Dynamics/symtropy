# Seedworks Regional Civilization Slice

## Working Title

**The Basin Wakes**

## Decision Summary

The first playable proof of Symtropy is one interconnected region, not one waterworks mission.

Old Waterworks remains an authored site and one possible crisis path. It no longer carries the burden of representing the entire game.

## Product Thesis

```text
A region is alive.
Its systems are connected.
Its people are becoming a society.
Your actions change what futures remain possible.
```

## Target Experience

```text
Length: 60–120 minutes for first complete run
Players: solo or 2–4 cooperative
Geography: one settlement hub, one open basin, four major sites
Persistence: one regional state snapshot and Chronicle summary
Replay: at least three materially different opening priorities
```

## Required Fantasies

The player should experience:

```text
I crossed a dangerous, beautiful place.
I discovered something that changed my plan.
I used tools, weapons, cargo, and movement.
I changed a physical system.
People noticed what I did.
The region now has a different future.
There is much more beyond the basin.
```

## Forbidden Reduction

The slice must not feel like only:

```text
a water repair tutorial
a dialogue-heavy civic hearing
a linear mission corridor
a factory automation demo
a combat arena with lore screens
```

## Firstlight Basin

Firstlight Basin is a damaged but inhabited regional system containing:

```text
Seedworks Outpost
Old Waterworks and riparian wetlands
a broken solar-thermal ridge
a rogue fabrication complex
a collapsed bridge and stranded convoy
a greenhouse and seed nursery
an archive/communications relay
subterranean utility passages
visible orbital wreckage
```

The basin should support multiple lines of movement and more than one sensible first objective.

## The Basin Cascade

A geomagnetic storm and dormant Null wake event create a regional cascade.

### Simultaneous Pressures

```text
Outpost reserve power is falling.
The medbay needs stable cooling.
The greenhouse climate loop is overheating.
Water pressure is declining.
The east bridge has collapsed with a convoy beyond it.
The archive relay is broadcasting contradictory identity records.
The rogue factory is manufacturing unrequested components.
Wildlife is leaving the willow wetlands.
An orbital fragment is predicted to cross the basin.
```

The player cannot fully solve every problem in the first run.

The opening decision establishes priority, not permanent ideology.

## Opening Sequence

### Beat 0 — Storm Descent

The player arrives or wakes during the storm.

Immediate actions:

```text
move through unstable shelter
help one injured person or recover personal gear
raise the Field Deck
observe multiple alarms without receiving a complete answer
```

The sky establishes the horizon: orbital debris, distant launch glow, and a signal that does not belong to the local storm.

### Beat 1 — The Living Hub

The player crosses Seedworks Outpost before choosing a mission.

The outpost must show:

```text
people working
children and elders sheltered
a food stall closing because refrigeration is failing
a mechanic cannibalizing a rover
a musician or ritual keeper calming the shelter
a robot denied access to one civic terminal
a trader protecting stranded cargo contracts
```

The hub introduces civilization through ordinary life, not a policy lecture.

### Beat 2 — Choose a First Commitment

The player selects or physically commits to one of five regional threads.

The others continue under NPC control and may worsen, stabilize, or create later rescue calls.

## Thread A — Reopen the Route

### Fantasy

```text
Movement is survival.
```

### Objective

Reach the collapsed east bridge, recover the stranded convoy, and restore a temporary crossing or alternate route.

### Primary Pillars

```text
Traverse
Contend
Coordinate
Transform
```

### Gameplay

```text
repair or commandeer a rover
navigate storm damage
fight or evade Null scout machines
use a bridge-layer, winch, or fabricated span
triage convoy cargo
choose what returns first
```

### Consequences

Restoring the route improves:

```text
food
medicine
materials
migration capacity
regional trust
```

It may also open the basin to raiders, corporate recovery crews, or displaced people.

## Thread B — Wake the Fabricator

### Fantasy

```text
Build the capacity to build.
```

### Objective

Enter the rogue fabrication complex and decide whether to restart, constrain, redesign, or strip its production line.

### Primary Pillars

```text
Investigate
Contend
Transform
```

### Gameplay

```text
industrial traversal
machine enemies
conveyor and crane hazards
blueprint recovery
line reconfiguration
physical component assembly
```

### Discovery

The factory is producing parts for an obsolete emergency-defense doctrine. Some parts are useful for the settlement.

### Consequences

A functioning line accelerates:

```text
repair
construction
robotics
weapons
vehicle recovery
```

It also increases:

```text
power demand
pollution
labor conflict
Null exposure
strategic attention
```

## Thread C — Stabilize the Living Systems

### Fantasy

```text
The ecosystem is not background.
```

### Objective

Investigate the greenhouse heat spike and the migration of wetland species.

### Primary Pillars

```text
Investigate
Transform
Belong
```

### Gameplay

```text
sample soil and water
follow animal behavior
repair greenhouse controls
redirect heat or water
deploy living filtration
discover an ecological signal beneath the instrumentation
```

### Discovery

The willow wetland is interacting with an old subterranean heat exchange network. It may be adapting, damaged, or beginning to express nonhuman agency.

### Consequences

The player can prioritize:

```text
crop survival
wetland continuity
water intake
scientific observation
rapid sterilization
```

No option should reduce to “nature good, technology bad.”

## Thread D — Restore the Signal

### Fantasy

```text
A civilization that cannot communicate cannot coordinate.
```

### Objective

Reach the ridge relay and resolve contradictory archive and identity broadcasts.

### Primary Pillars

```text
Traverse
Investigate
Coordinate
```

### Gameplay

```text
climb exposed terrain
deploy a mobile signal mast
align physical antenna elements
defend the calibration
compare live and archived records
choose which channels to restore
```

### Discovery

The relay contains a fragment from an earlier worldline or an anomalous off-world source. It is not yet clear whether this is corruption, evidence, or contact.

### Consequences

Signal restoration unlocks:

```text
regional map clarity
convoy coordination
remote device access
new NPC arrivals
Chronicle replication
future contact
```

It may also expose the settlement to surveillance.

## Thread E — Old Waterworks

### Fantasy

```text
Repairing infrastructure means entering history.
```

### Objective

Restore safe water flow through a system still enforcing dead authority.

### Primary Pillars

```text
Investigate
Transform
Coordinate
```

### Gameplay

Preserve the strongest material from:

```text
ops/SEEDWORKS_PLAYABLE_SLICE_SPEC.md
lore/OLD_WATERWORKS_LEVEL_BIBLE.md
```

The site should include physical repair, machine resistance, wetland consequences, and at least one non-bureaucratic solution.

### Consequences

Water improves health and settlement capacity, but restoration method creates different ownership, ecological, and trust outcomes.

## Cross-Thread Interactions

The five threads are not isolated quests.

Examples:

```text
A restored route brings the part needed for the waterworks.
A constrained factory fabricates the bridge anchor.
The signal relay locates the convoy.
The wetland provides a low-energy filtration alternative.
Water cooling keeps the fabricator stable.
Factory power demand worsens medbay risk.
```

The slice should author a bounded set of cross-links rather than simulate every possibility.

## The First Major Threat

The first recurring enemy family is a **Null Emergency Fabric**, distributed across machines that still coordinate around a dead continuity doctrine.

It can inhabit:

```text
factory crawlers
relay defense units
waterworks locks
route-control drones
diagnostic systems
```

Each body should have a distinct tactical role.

### Tactical Roles

```text
Scout — marks players and calls reinforcement.
Anchor — establishes a defended zone.
Carrier — transports parts or hazards.
Jammer — disrupts Field Deck and team signal.
Custodian — repairs other machines and closes routes.
Heavy Interdictor — site boss or pursuit threat.
```

Null must be fun to fight before its philosophy is fully understood.

## Construction Proof

The slice must include one persistent constructed object chosen by the player:

```text
temporary bridge
relay mast
fabricator workcell
greenhouse heat exchanger
water bypass
defensive gate
```

The object must:

```text
exist physically
change navigation or production
have a visible condition
appear in the return state
```

## Mobility Proof

The player must use at least one mobility transition:

```text
on foot to rover
rover to climb
cargo vehicle to deployed platform
```

The vehicle may be damaged, borrowed, public, or assembled. It should feel like capability, not merely faster walking.

## Cultural Proof

Before the final resolution, include one scene that is neither crisis meeting nor tutorial:

```text
shared meal
storm song
repair ritual
children painting route markers
quiet memorial
competitive tool demonstration
dance or festival preparation
```

The scene changes according to regional pressure and gives the player a reason to care about more than metrics.

## Resolution: The Basin Council Is Not the Finale

The player returns to a changed hub.

The resolution includes:

```text
visible physical changes
NPC routines responding
one concise coordination decision
one relationship consequence
one Chronicle summary
new opportunities opened by the chosen thread
```

A mandatory long hearing is prohibited.

Governance should compress consequence, not consume the entire ending.

## Chronicle Output

The first Chronicle should record:

```text
what the player prioritized
what was physically changed
who or what was preserved
what deteriorated elsewhere
what precedent or relationship emerged
what unresolved signal remains
```

Example:

```text
During the Basin Wake, the eastern route was reopened before the outpost fabricator was stabilized. The convoy returned with medicine and twelve displaced passengers. The factory continued its emergency production unattended.
```

## Replay Structure

A second run should differ through:

```text
different first thread
different origin emphasis
different route and vehicle availability
different crisis deterioration
different NPC coalition
one shifted Null deployment
```

Avoid randomizing away authored meaning.


## Acceptance Tests

The slice passes only if playtesters can answer:

```text
What kind of game is Symtropy?
What did you choose to do first?
What physically changed?
What did you discover?
What was fun to control?
Who or what did you care about?
What do you want to reach beyond the basin?
```

If most answers mention only a pump, the slice has failed.
