# v1.4 Planetary Society Campaign

## Purpose

The v1.4 campaign fills the scale gap between mature settlements and full planetary civilization.

Earlier Symtropy documents define settlements, charters, factions, war, worldlines, ecology, trade integrity, mobility, orbital habitats, and long-term continuity. v1.4 specifies the institutions and physical networks that let many distinct communities coordinate a planet without becoming one centralized empire or a decorative strategy layer.

## Governing Principles

```text
coordinate without homogenizing
share authority without making it permanent
move matter without teleportation
trade without confusing currency for inventory
adapt climate without erasing local burden
reach orbit without hiding surface cost
permit exit without erasing interdependence
meet nonhuman worlds without inventing one spokesperson
```

## Scope Boundary

v1.4 does not move planetary federation into Seedworks scope.

The representative game still proves one region. Planetary systems advance only through bounded regional fixtures and evidence gates.

## Implementation Claim Boundary

All new systems remain `I0 — not assessed`.

No implementation, performance, scalability, or player-comprehension claim follows from these documents alone.

## Required Evidence

```text
schemas and code ownership
conservation and authority property tests
deterministic replay
save/load and schema migration
worldline fork tests
regional fixtures
performance traces
human comprehension studies
failure and recovery evidence
```

## Result

The corpus now distinguishes:

```text
settlement governance
regional interdependence
planetary shared systems
orbital-planetary interface
worldline ancestry and forks
```

That hierarchy allows the galaxy to become large without making every scale feel like the same city-management screen.
