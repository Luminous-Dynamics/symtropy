# v1.3 Civilization Continuity Campaign

## Goal

Make Symtropy capable of describing and eventually proving how a civilization survives the loss of founders, expertise, records, infrastructure, homes, and inherited certainty across decades.

## Patch Groups

1. civic succession, public service, and institutional continuity;
2. administration, authority, procurement, corruption, and succession runtime;
3. archives, historiography, heritage, and collective memory;
4. evidence provenance, custody, access, correction, and archive runtime;
5. disaster preparedness, continuity of operations, and recovery;
6. warnings, evacuation, shelter, relief, incident coordination, and recovery runtime;
7. cultural evolution, language, and intergenerational transmission;
8. demography, skill succession, cohorts, language, and cultural evolution runtime;
9. fifty-year integrated civilization-continuity benchmark;
10. canon, roadmap, readiness, registry, manifest, audit, and patch integration.

## Representative Proof

One Firstlight district runs for fifty simulated years through ordinary life, two leadership transitions, migration, a major disaster, recovery, archival dispute, elder knowledge loss, youth cultural change, player absence, and a two-branch worldline fork.

## Product Claim Boundary

This campaign permits the claim that Symtropy has a bounded design and validation architecture for civilizational continuity.

It does not permit claims that the systems are implemented, historically predictive, sociologically complete, legally prescriptive, or capable of autonomously governing real communities.

## Kill Discipline

Remove or simplify any component that fails to improve player comprehension, continuity, or meaningful long-horizon consequence relative to deterministic baseline—or that causes critical authority, privacy, accessibility, performance, persistence, or authoring regressions.
