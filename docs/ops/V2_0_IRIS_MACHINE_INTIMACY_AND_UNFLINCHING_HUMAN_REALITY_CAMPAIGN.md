# v2.0 IRIS, Machine Intimacy, and Unflinching Human Reality Campaign

## Goal

Define IRIS as the Field Deck's bounded cognitive companion and establish the systems required for Symtropy to portray difficult bodily, intimate, social, psychological, and wartime realities without exploitation.

## Patch Groups

1. IRIS identity and authority.
2. IRIS cognition, memory, voice, source chain, and historical lineages.
3. Private bodily life, disability, aging, hygiene, and care.
4. Adult sexuality, intimacy, sex work, reproductive autonomy, and coercion.
5. Addiction, intoxication, harm reduction, and pharmaceutical dependence.
6. Domestic coercive control, escape, and survivor agency.
7. War atrocity, detention, displacement, and postwar accountability.
8. Mental health, faith, grief, and ambiguous experience.
9. Grounded scene anthology and mature-content production standard.
10. Integrated benchmark, fixtures, canon, roadmap, readiness, registry, and audit.

## Non-Goals

v2.0 does not:

- claim IRIS is conscious;
- require Symthaea terminology in player-facing lore;
- ship explicit sexual content;
- create torture or assault mechanics;
- make mature content mandatory;
- simulate every bodily process;
- replace clinicians, courts, faith communities, or player judgment with IRIS;
- promote any capability beyond documentation readiness.

## Proof Order

```text
deterministic IRIS core
→ privacy and authority harness
→ one bounded care scene
→ one adult boundary scene
→ one harm-reduction fixture
→ one coercive-control safety plan
→ one war-evidence aftermath
→ one mental-health and faith ambiguity scene
→ IRIS integrity failure and fork
→ integrated fourteen-day benchmark
```

## Release Claim Boundary

The campaign establishes `D3 / I0` design readiness only.

No claim of clinical realism, therapeutic safety, autonomous companionship, conscious AI, comprehensive trauma representation, or finished mature content is supported until code, subject review, accessibility, localization, privacy, performance, multiplayer, persistence, and playtest evidence exists.

## Cut Rule

If a mature system cannot preserve consent, privacy, ordinary life, player control, and aftermath, reduce it to a less invasive form or cut it.

If IRIS cannot function safely without generative output, ship the deterministic assistant instead.

## Campaign Principle

> **The brave choice is not to show everything. It is to refuse the convenient lie that bodies, desire, dependency, coercion, faith, and grief stop mattering when gameplay begins.**
