# Seedworks Production Budget and Content Plan

## Owned Question

**What is the smallest production package that can honestly represent Symtropy without becoming a one-system prototype or an impossible miniature MMO?**

## Core Thesis

The representative build should be broad in **interaction** and narrow in **content volume**.

```text
Few places, many relationships.
Few tools, deep combination.
Few threats, strong tactical roles.
Few NPCs, credible lives.
Few resource chains, real geography.
One region, several futures.
```

## Product Target

Build one polished Firstlight Basin region capable of supporting:

```text
45–90 minute representative sessions
5–10 hours of distinct first-play progression
replay through alternate opening commitments
solo and 2–4 player cooperative play
persistent regional consequences
```

This is not a promise of commercial launch content volume. It is the production proof from which expansion can be measured.

## Content Budget Philosophy

Every content item must serve at least two functions.

Examples:

```text
A bridge is traversal + logistics + tactical space + regional consequence.
A greenhouse is ecology + care + construction + social life.
A relay tower is navigation + climbing + communication + horizon reveal.
A rover is mobility + cargo + identity + cooperative stations.
```

Single-purpose content requires exceptional value.

## Region Budget

### Geography

Target:

```text
1 coherent basin
1 primary settlement hub
5 major authored sites
6–10 minor discoveries or activity pockets
3 traversal bands: foot, ground vehicle, elevated/technical route
2–3 weather states with gameplay effect
```

The region must feel geographically connected rather than like five isolated missions.

### Major Sites

Recommended set:

```text
1. Firstlight Outpost
2. Collapsed Route and Convoy Zone
3. Rogue Fabricator / Factory Complex
4. Living Systems or Greenhouse-Wetland Site
5. Signal Ridge / Relay Infrastructure
6. Old Waterworks
```

The hub counts as a major site; five external threads may be built incrementally.

### Site State Variants

Each major external site should support:

```text
initial crisis state
one transformed success state
one partial/failure or alternate state
```

Do not author entirely separate levels for every outcome. Use systemic state changes, modular dressing, NPC routines, device topology, access routes, and threat posture.

## NPC Budget

### Named NPCs

Target initial representative set:

```text
8–12 named NPCs
```

Required role coverage:

```text
settlement coordination
repair/fabrication
care
logistics/vehicle
science/ecology
defense
culture/everyday life
machine or nonhuman participant
outsider/faction contact
```

Each named NPC needs:

```text
recognizable voice and silhouette
daily routine
one relationship outside the player
one protected value
one blind spot
one response to each major thread outcome
one leisure or private-life behavior
```

### Ambient Population

Target:

```text
20–40 lower-fidelity residents across schedules and state variants
```

Use role and schedule systems, not unique dialogue trees for everyone.

## Threat Budget

Target:

```text
1 primary Null ecology family
5–7 tactical role variants
2 environmental hazard families
1 human or social conflict that may escalate without becoming the main enemy army
1 boss-scale system encounter
```

Role variants should share locomotion, materials, and animation where possible while changing tactical behavior.

Avoid building several unrelated enemy factions before one encounter family feels excellent.

## Player Equipment Budget

### Tools

Target:

```text
Field Deck
multi-role repair/assembly tool
cutting or breach tool
sampling/diagnostic tool
cargo handling aid
```

Tools should have alternate functions and meaningful physical feedback.

### Weapons and Defense

Target:

```text
2 primary weapon families
1 industrial-tool combat path
1 defensive/de-escalatory family
2 throwable or deployable devices
```

This is enough to test handling, tactical combination, and nonlethal alternatives without attempting a full arsenal.

### Vehicles

Target:

```text
1 player-operable ground rover with cargo and crew roles
1 non-player or limited-use heavy logistics vehicle
1 horizon-visible aircraft or orbital vehicle
```

The ground rover should receive deep handling, damage, cargo, customization, and cooperative support before a broad vehicle catalog is implemented.

## Construction Budget

Target buildable families:

```text
1 route structure: bridge or modular span
1 utility structure: relay, power bridge, or fluid conduit
1 productive structure: workshop/fabricator module
1 habitat or cultural structure: shelter room, market element, or gathering installation
1 defensive structure: sensor, barrier, or bounded emplacement
```

Construction must prove:

```text
survey
material staging
assembly
commissioning
quality
persistent use
```

Freeform city construction is deferred until modular construction feels tactile and consequential.

## Resource and Economy Budget

Target:

```text
3 core material families
2 energy carriers
1 food/living-system chain
1 precision component chain
1 knowledge/blueprint chain
1 transport-capacity constraint
```

Example:

```text
scrap metal → cleaned stock → structural component
salvaged electronics → tested modules → control hardware
biomass/water/energy → greenhouse output
```

Each chain must connect to geography, storage, transport, and at least one social or ecological consequence.

Avoid more than 12–16 actively relevant item types in the first hour.

## Field Deck Screen Budget

Representative build requires:

```text
SCAN
basic map/navigation
DIAG
REPAIR
SHARE/ping
compact Chronicle recap
```

Contextual or partial support:

```text
ARCHIVE
CIVIC
NULL
WITNESS
TACTICAL NET
```

Do not build every deep mode to final scope before the core physical interaction is proven.

## Cultural and Everyday-Life Budget

Required:

```text
1 shared meal or market sequence
1 local music/performance identity
1 playful vehicle/traversal activity
1 customizable personal or crew space
1 post-crisis gathering not centered on governance
1 recurring NPC joke, rivalry, or social ritual
```

These are representative product content, not optional end-stage polish.

## Narrative Budget

### Authored Story

Target:

```text
1 regional cascade
5 opening thread arcs
1 convergent major threat
2–3 faction interpretations
1 cosmic-horizon mystery
```

### Dialogue

Prefer reactive short-form dialogue and situational conversation over long branching trees.

Budget unique long-form scenes for:

```text
opening arrival
first return
major threat resolution
one cultural gathering
one horizon reveal
```

## Audio Budget

Required audio families:

```text
player body and tools
rover
weather
settlement states
machine ecology roles
major site signatures
Field Deck attention hierarchy
music/performance layer
```

Machines should be diagnosable by sound before all visual UI is complete.

## Art and Environment Budget

Use a coherent Firstlight material language with modular families:

```text
settlement repair architecture
public works ruins
industrial machine ecology
living restoration systems
regional transport infrastructure
```

Each major site needs:

```text
one dominant silhouette
one navigational landmark
one before/after visual state
one intimate human-scale detail set
```

Avoid spending the budget on distant megastructures that cannot be approached.

## Animation Budget

Prioritize:

```text
hands and tools
locomotion and mantling
rover entry/exit and crew stations
core NPC work/leisure loops
machine threat readability
team carry/handoff
```

Defer broad emote catalogs and many creature skeletons until these are strong.

## Performance Budgets

Exact numbers depend on target hardware, but every milestone must declare:

```text
frame-time budget by major system
active physics entity budget
AI agent fidelity tiers
network replication budget
event volume budget
memory budget
save size and load target
```

Representative minimum performance proof:

```text
stable target frame rate during major threat + NPC + device activity
no unbounded event cascades
region simulation runs while one site is active
save/load preserves transformed states
2–4 player session remains responsive under representative load
```

## Milestone Plan

### M0 — Walking Region

Required:

```text
basin graybox
hub and two external sites
foot traversal
basic interaction
weather and landmarks
```

Proof:

Players can navigate without objective arrows and voluntarily detour.

### M1 — Hands, Rover, and Threat

Required:

```text
tool handling
Field Deck SCAN
rover
one threat family with three roles
body damage and recovery
```

Proof:

Moving, driving, and contact are enjoyable before deeper simulation.

### M2 — Transform One Route and One Site

Required:

```text
cargo movement
modular construction
one device network
persistent state
NPC return response
```

Proof:

Players see a physical change alter regional possibility.

### M3 — Three Thread Representative Build

Required threads:

```text
route/convoy
fabricator/industry
living systems or signal
```

Proof:

Different roles and routes produce distinct sessions within the same region.

### M4 — Living Return

Required:

```text
NPC routines and memory
settlement state response
cultural activity
basic Chronicle
```

Proof:

Players return voluntarily and recognize consequence without a summary screen.

### M5 — Full Five-Thread Slice

Add remaining threads and convergent threat.

Proof:

Replay, role fairness, solo/co-op, and content budgets pass.

## Cut Lines

### Cut First

```text
additional enemy factions
second fully operable vehicle family
advanced governance UI
full terminal scripting
large freeform building catalog
many origins
advanced alien contact
planetary simulation detail
```

### Protect Until Late

```text
excellent movement and tools
rover handling
three distinct opening threads
living return response
one cultural activity
persistent transformation
clear horizon
```

### Never Cut From Representative Claim

```text
player choice among activities
meaningful danger
construction or transformation
resource movement
NPC life
world consequence
```

If these are missing, call the build a subsystem prototype rather than a Symtropy slice.

## Content Multiplication Strategy

Build reusable grammars for:

```text
site state transitions
cargo contracts
threat role composition
NPC interpretation
construction commissioning
regional network changes
Field Deck evidence
Chronicle summaries
```

Do not use procedural generation to compensate for weak authored foundations.

First author one excellent example. Then generalize what actually repeats.

## Production Review Questions

For every proposed feature:

```text
Which player fantasy does it serve?
Which existing content does it combine with?
What can be cut if it enters scope?
What acceptance evidence proves it?
What is its update/runtime budget?
What happens in solo and co-op?
Does it create replay or only volume?
```

## Representative Build Acceptance Gates

```text
The region supports at least three distinct 45–90 minute session shapes.
The first hour contains no more than 16 actively relevant inventory items.
The rover, tools, and primary threat are enjoyable in isolation.
At least one transformation alters route, NPC routine, and regional state.
At least three player roles contribute visibly in co-op.
Solo players can complete the same causal activities with bounded support.
Players encounter culture or leisure before the final resolution.
Performance remains stable in the most integrated representative scene.
The team can identify a clear cut list without damaging the game constitution.
```

## Final Rule

```text
Scope breadth through interacting systems.
Scope discipline through limited content families.
```
