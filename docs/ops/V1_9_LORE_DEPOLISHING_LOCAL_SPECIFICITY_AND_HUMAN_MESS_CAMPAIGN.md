# v1.9 Lore De-Polishing, Local Specificity, and Human Mess Campaign

## Objective

Preserve Symtropy's ethical and causal foundations while making its civilizations less rhetorically uniform, less concept-pure, and more recognizably inhabited.

The campaign responds to a tone audit that found strong substance but growing stylistic symmetry:

```text
poetic-name saturation
balanced corporate templates
figures with perfectly paired virtues and harms
societies organized too cleanly around one thesis
lore prose explaining its moral too directly
insufficient vanity, rivalry, faith, boredom, and incompetence
too little low-prestige popular culture
uniformly admirable diasporas and benevolent shadow networks
historical events with excessively efficient consequences
nonhuman life appearing mainly during contact dilemmas
```

## What this campaign does not do

It does not:

- weaken the rights floor;
- make consent, privacy, or evidence culturally optional;
- replace causal simulation with random personality;
- turn serious cultures into parody;
- expose private histories merely to create texture;
- force human emotional labels onto nonhuman agency;
- authorize implementation maturity beyond `I0`;
- expand production beyond the one-region playable-history proof.

## Production target

The first implementation target remains Nine Pumps and its connected region.

The v1.9 layer should make that region feel inhabited through:

- multiple names in actual use;
- an Aureline legacy that is competent, loved, resented, and legally messy;
- ordinary artifacts;
- unrelated institutions;
- sport, cheap food, bad media, and generational slang;
- public and private motives;
- disputed history;
- at least one nonhuman or ecological pattern not centered on the player.

## Governing principle

> **Symtropy's constitutional values belong in the systems and boundaries. Its inhabitants do not need to speak as though they have read the design bible.**
