# Symtropy Game Implementation Roadmap

## Strategy

The project should not attempt to implement every documented civilization scale simultaneously.

The recommended sequence is:

```text
Firstlight / Nine Pumps causal spine
→ one functioning mixed-use pleasure district
→ deep profession infrastructure proven across twelve jobs
→ generalize the profession framework to the wider game
→ expand planetary and interstellar systems only after representative evidence
```

# Gate 0 — Preserve the Existing Representative Spine

Required before v2.1 implementation:

- authoritative world state;
- stable identities;
- device and transaction validation;
- named NPC continuity;
- work orders and consequences;
- save/load and replay;
- IRIS evidence boundary;
- privacy and consent foundations.

# Gate 1 — District Metabolism

Implement:

- venue schedules and capacities;
- visitor demand;
- staffing and pay;
- water, power, cooling, sanitation, transport, housing, and medical capacity;
- day/night/closing/cleanup scheduler;
- district-level simulation.

Proof: a district cannot operate without real labor and infrastructure.

# Gate 2 — Three Foundational Professions

Implement:

1. sanitation worker;
2. nightlife medic;
3. maintenance technician.

These prove:

- sensory cues;
- tools and calibration;
- shifts;
- handovers;
- material consequences;
- care and privacy;
- cleanup and aftermath.

# Gate 3 — Gambling and Public Trust

Implement:

- wagers, reserves, odds, settlement, and insolvency;
- dealer;
- gambling auditor;
- licensing inspector;
- evidence and appeal.

Proof: no money duplication and no automatic guilt.

# Gate 4 — Substance and Nightlife Safety

Implement:

- product batches;
- testing;
- intoxication interactions;
- harm-reduction services;
- public alerts;
- de-escalation;
- safe transport.

Proof: care can operate without criminalizing patients or leaking private data.

# Gate 5 — Culture and Service

Implement:

- performer;
- hospitality;
- transit;
- substance steward;
- cultural schedule;
- visitor and resident reputations;
- ordinary positive experiences.

Proof: the district is worth protecting, not merely a harm simulator.

# Gate 6 — Adult Venue

Implement only after adulthood, consent, worker sovereignty, privacy, content controls, subject review, moderation, and safe fallback exist.

Use non-explicit authored presentation.

Proof: a worker may refuse, work voluntarily, organize, change services, preserve privacy, and leave without becoming a rescue reward.

# Gate 7 — Informal Markets and Corruption

Implement:

- informal-service networks;
- regulation and inspections;
- corruption relationships;
- selective enforcement;
- service substitution;
- public decisions.

Proof: destroying an organization does not erase the needs it served.

# Gate 8 — Seven-Day Integrated Benchmark

Run:

- all twelve profession anchors;
- festival week;
- product alert;
- gambling reserve issue;
- infrastructure failure;
- adult-worker refusal;
- corruption investigation;
- cleanup and Monday aftermath;
- player absence;
- three worldline branches.

# Gate 9 — Generalize Professions

Only after the benchmark passes, extract reusable profession infrastructure for:

- construction;
- medicine;
- ecology;
- logistics;
- governance;
- archives;
- exploration;
- robotics;
- spacecraft;
- diplomacy.

The wider profession campaign should reuse proven structures while preserving distinct tempo and sensory language.

# Stop Conditions

Pause expansion if:

- professions remain distinguishable only by labels or animation;
- the district requires the player to solve every event;
- content safety cannot preserve worker agency and privacy;
- performance requires deleting authoritative labor or consent state;
- the system produces chores without judgment;
- profession authoring cost exceeds sustainable production budgets;
- ordinary life and delight disappear beneath crisis systems.

# Promotion Evidence

Every gate requires:

- implementation commit;
- schema and migration version;
- deterministic replay;
- save/load test;
- performance trace;
- privacy and rights review;
- accessibility and localization review;
- playtest evidence;
- known limitations;
- rollback or kill switch.

## Roadmap Summary

The next implementation target is not “all professions” and not “a giant sin city.”

It is one district where twelve kinds of work remain distinct, consequential, and worth returning to on Monday morning.
