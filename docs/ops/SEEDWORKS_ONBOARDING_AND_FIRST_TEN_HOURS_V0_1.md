# Seedworks Onboarding and First Ten Hours

## Owned Question

**How does a new player enter Symtropy without a lore dump, a municipal checklist, or a wall of interacting systems?**

## Core Thesis

Onboarding should teach Symtropy through a sequence of widening capabilities:

```text
I can move and act.
I can read enough of the world to make a choice.
I can survive pressure.
I can transform something physical.
I can work with others.
I can choose a role.
I can open a route into a larger future.
```

The first ten hours should reveal complexity in layers while preserving freedom and mystery.

## Onboarding Principles

### 1. Teach Through Consequence

A tutorial action should produce a visible world response.

Do not teach a system through a detached training room unless accessibility or control calibration requires it.

### 2. One New Question at a Time

The player may encounter several systems, but the game should ask them to actively reason about only one unfamiliar question at a time.

### 3. Show Horizon Before Detail

The player can see ships, cities, orbital wreckage, advanced machines, cultural factions, and worldline scars before understanding how they work.

Visible complexity creates aspiration. Interactive complexity must be staged.

### 4. The First Choice Must Be Real but Recoverable

The player’s first commitment changes timing, relationships, and local conditions without permanently locking the entire campaign.

### 5. No Required Lore Comprehension

Players may understand immediate stakes before understanding historical causes.

The game should reward deeper reading without punishing those who act first and investigate later.

### 6. Multiple Player Fantasies Must Appear Early

The opening must not imply that every player is fundamentally a repair technician.

Within the first hour, players should see credible futures as:

```text
explorer
builder
fighter
driver
scientist
logistician
medic
social participant
```

## Entry Configuration

Before the world begins, allow concise configuration:

```text
input and accessibility setup
motion and camera comfort
combat assistance
dialogue/text density
interaction hold/toggle preference
solo or cooperative start
optional origin selection
```

Origins should change emphasis, dialogue, and recognition—not require reading several pages before play.

Recommended origin presentation:

```text
one-sentence history
one visible strength
one complication
one starting relationship or obligation
```

Deeper biography remains optional.

## The First 10 Minutes

### Promise

```text
I am in a real place.
I can move competently.
Something is happening now.
My first action helps.
The world is larger than this crisis.
```

### Sequence

#### 0:00–2:00 — Arrival Under Pressure

The player arrives during the Firstlight Basin storm cascade.

They see:

```text
unstable weather
settlement lights failing in sections
movement in the distance
orbital debris or a damaged structure in the sky
people already responding
```

Required interactions:

```text
move
look
mantle or cross one obstacle
pick up or equip one tool
```

No codex panel interrupts arrival.

#### 2:00–5:00 — Immediate Useful Action

The player performs one short embodied intervention:

```text
clear a jammed shelter door
secure a loose power lead
help move an injured person
stabilize a vehicle
raise a local signal marker
```

This teaches:

```text
interaction targeting
brief sustained action
clear success feedback
NPC acknowledgment
```

The action should not be a full technical puzzle.

#### 5:00–8:00 — Field Deck as Instrument

The player raises the Field Deck for one bounded question.

The Deck reveals:

```text
what is physically present
one immediate fault or route signal
confidence and missing information
```

Do not introduce every mode.

Available initially:

```text
SCAN
basic map
simple share/ping
```

DIAG appears only after connection to a real system.

#### 8:00–10:00 — First Commitment

The player reaches the living hub and sees several urgent threads.

Presentation should be spatial and social:

```text
stranded convoy signal
smoke or machine activity near the rogue factory
greenhouse overheating
relay mast dark
water pressure falling
```

NPCs describe consequences, not quest categories.

The player chooses where to go first.

## The First 30 Minutes

### Promise

```text
My route matters.
I can handle danger.
I can change a physical system.
Other people are doing things without waiting for me.
```

Required experiences:

```text
regional traversal
one pressure or threat encounter
one physical transformation
one NPC relationship beat
one discovery not required by the objective
```

### Thread Adaptation

Any opening thread should teach the same foundational contracts through different content.

#### Route/Convoy Thread

Teaches:

```text
vehicle interaction
cargo and route legibility
team coordination
terrain hazard
```

#### Fabricator Thread

Teaches:

```text
salvage
machine connection
assembly or configuration
industrial threat
```

#### Living Systems Thread

Teaches:

```text
sampling
habitat condition
uncertainty
care and ecology
```

#### Signal Thread

Teaches:

```text
navigation
climbing or high-ground traversal
network visibility
threat detection
```

#### Waterworks Thread

Teaches:

```text
physical diagnosis
repair path choice
device authority
```

No thread should teach every advanced layer.

## The First Hour

### Promise

```text
I completed a meaningful expedition.
The settlement changed when I returned.
I know several things I could do next.
```

Required by the end of hour one:

```text
one opening thread completed or meaningfully failed
one persistent regional or settlement change
one equipment or knowledge gain
one relationship memory
one visible consequence in NPC routine or environment
one calm social or scenic beat
```

### Return Sequence

The return to Firstlight must not be only a reward screen.

Show:

```text
cargo unloading
lights or facilities changing
NPCs moving differently
new conversation fragments
one opportunity created by the result
one unresolved cost
```

A short Chronicle or Field Deck recap may summarize after the world has already shown the change.

## Hours 1–2: Role Discovery

### Promise

```text
I can choose how I contribute.
```

The player gains access to at least three role-signaling opportunities:

```text
field expedition
construction or fabrication
combat/defense
logistics/vehicle work
science/ecology
care/social support
```

Role discovery comes from doing, not a class menu.

Provide one low-risk trial activity for each major role.

Examples:

```text
short rover delivery
optional ruin survey
workshop assembly order
perimeter defense alert
clinic supply triage
wildlife observation task
```

The player may change direction without penalty.

## Hours 2–5: Regional Interdependence

### Promise

```text
The basin is connected.
Opening one possibility changes another.
```

The player should:

```text
complete a second major thread
use a vehicle or major mobility transition
participate in one multi-stage construction or operation
see one system consequence propagate across domains
meet a faction or culture beyond the starting hub
```

Examples of propagation:

```text
Bridge reopened → trade arrives → workshop gains material → threat adapts to route.
Signal restored → weather warning improves → expedition becomes possible → outside group makes contact.
Fabricator awakened → repair capacity rises → labor dispute or public-access question emerges.
```

Introduce:

```text
DIAG
REPAIR
basic logistics view
relationship-specific access
```

Keep ARCHIVE, CIVIC, NULL, and deep terminal work contextual rather than permanently active.

## Hours 5–10: Ownership and Horizon

### Promise

```text
I have changed this region and begun a future of my own.
```

By hour ten, the player should have:

```text
a preferred role or combination of roles
one personally meaningful tool, vehicle, space, or companion
one durable infrastructure capability
one relationship with trust, rivalry, obligation, or affection
one significant threat encounter
one self-directed discovery
one cultural or leisure activity
one decision with visible historical potential
one glimpse of orbital, planetary, alien, or worldline scale
```

The horizon reveal may be:

```text
a recovered star map
an orbital transmission
a falling ship component
an alien-pattern anomaly
a distant city request
a worldline contradiction
```

It should create a question, not immediately unlock the whole galaxy.

## Complexity Reveal Schedule

### Immediate

```text
movement
basic interaction
simple tool use
SCAN
map/ping
body danger
```

### First Thread

```text
one vehicle or traversal system
one device or ecological system
basic combat or avoidance
simple cargo/material handling
```

### First Return

```text
persistent world change
NPC memory
basic Chronicle summary
```

### Hours 1–3

```text
DIAG and REPAIR
construction stages
role trials
resource geography
relationship access
```

### Hours 3–6

```text
basic automation
trade or contracts
faction interpretation
regional simulation
advanced Field Deck context as needed
```

### Hours 6–10

```text
specialization
public/civic consequence
stronger construction
coordinated threat
cosmic horizon
```

Do not reveal a mode because the tutorial schedule says so. Reveal it when the world creates a question that the mode answers.

## Tutorial Delivery Surfaces

Use in priority order:

```text
world affordance
animation and sound
NPC demonstration
short contextual prompt
Field Deck question chip
optional detailed help
```

Avoid long modal tutorials during active danger.

## Assistance Model

Players may independently configure:

```text
interaction simplification
aim assistance
navigation guidance
objective detail
puzzle hints
diagnostic explanation
resource-chain visualization
combat lethality
```

Assistance should expose the same world state rather than fabricate a separate simplified canon.

## Returning Player Onboarding

After a long absence, provide:

```text
what changed while away
current body/equipment condition
active commitments
relationship changes
regional opportunities
control refresher option
```

Do not force a replay of the opening.

## Cooperative Onboarding

### New Player Joining Experienced World

Provide:

```text
baseline personal equipment
safe role trial
mentor or public-training access
context summary
meaningful contribution within 10 minutes
```

Do not require the new player to repeat low-level grind.

### Mixed Experience Group

Tutorial prompts are per player.

World actions remain shared.

Experienced players should not be able to accidentally complete every teaching interaction before the new player participates.

Use parallel work, personal observation, and protected first-use moments where appropriate.

## Onboarding Failure Continuation

Early mistakes should teach through recoverable consequences.

Examples:

```text
vehicle damaged → field repair or tow
wrong sample handling → lower confidence, not dead campaign
failed defense → route becomes riskier
poor construction → reduced quality and later maintenance
```

Avoid irreversible political or biological consequences before the player has enough information to understand responsibility.

## Anti-Patterns

Reject openings that:

```text
begin with several minutes of exposition
force every player through the same pump repair
unlock all Field Deck modes at once
present the basin only as quest markers
make the first combat encounter mechanically trivial but visually chaotic
require inventory management before the player has a goal
make the player the only competent person in the settlement
hold culture and delight until “after the game is finished”
```

## Onboarding Telemetry

Measure:

```text
time to first successful action
time to first voluntary detour
first chosen thread distribution
prompt dismissal and reread
Field Deck dwell time
first death or failure cause
number of verbs used in first hour
percentage of players who notice settlement change
role preference after two and ten hours
```

Telemetry must be interpreted with observation and interviews.

## First-Ten-Hour Acceptance Gates

```text
Most players perform a useful action within five minutes.
Players can choose a first thread without understanding all lore.
No opening thread is selected by more than 50% solely because the others are unclear.
Players use at least five distinct core verbs in the first hour.
Players can name one persistent change after returning.
Players identify at least two viable player fantasies by hour one.
Most players form a preferred role by hour five without feeling class-locked.
Players experience at least one enjoyable non-crisis activity before hour ten.
Players can state a self-directed next goal at the end of the test.
```

## Final Rule

```text
Onboarding should make the world feel larger as the player becomes more capable,
not smaller as the interface explains it.
```
