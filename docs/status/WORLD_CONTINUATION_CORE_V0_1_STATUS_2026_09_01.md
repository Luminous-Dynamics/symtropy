## Important semantics

A physical-state digest may serve as `resume_identity` only when the entry explicitly declares `ResumeIdentityClass::PhysicalState` and the two typed digests are identical.

Authorities with hidden continuation-significant state must use `ResumeIdentityClass::Continuation` with the stronger domain-owned continuation digest.

This prevents a narrower physical digest from being silently promoted into a stronger continuation claim.
