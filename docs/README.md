## v2.7 — The Breaking of Firstlight

The opening is now a bounded two-hour worldline-specific prologue. Players meet a unique generated cast, participate in ordinary Firstlight life, observe an anomalous warning, influence preparation and evacuation, and depart in a physically constrained Continuance Crawler carrying selected people, objects, records, ecology and unfinished obligations.

The stable settlement ends; its history does not. The first forty hours now grow from mobile survival, survivor life, travel, profession mastery, new settlements and eventual return.
