# Pleasure City Profession Packets

## Purpose

These twelve packets are the first proof that profession depth can produce distinct forms of play inside one coherent district.

They are not a complete job catalog. They are production references for sensory identity, shift rhythm, public responsibility, and mastery.

# 1. Dealer and Game Operator

## Core Fantasy

Maintain a fast, fair, socially charged game while reading probability, procedure, people, and the room.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- chip weight and stack geometry;
- card or device handling rhythm;
- payout-table state;
- hesitation and collusion cues;
- table pace;
- surveillance signals;
- intoxication and aggression changes;
- small discrepancies at reconciliation.

## Moment Loop

Handle the game accurately, announce state, resolve valid actions, preserve pace, and halt when integrity or participant capacity becomes uncertain.

## Shift Arc

Table setup, equipment verification, early quiet play, high-volume pressure, difficult guests, relief rotation, cash or token reconciliation, discrepancy report, transport home.

## Mastery Styles

- ceremonial precision;
- high-volume efficiency;
- social de-escalation;
- tournament rules expertise;
- advantage-play literacy;
- worker-organizing leadership.

## Failure With Depth

A dealer may make no mechanical error yet harm integrity by ignoring a supervisor’s pressure to keep an impaired profitable visitor playing.

# 2. Bartender or Substance Steward

## Core Fantasy

Create pleasure and hospitality while controlling composition, pacing, interaction risk, inventory, and social atmosphere.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- aroma, viscosity, color, temperature, carbonation, dose marks;
- customer pacing and coordination;
- contamination and storage cues;
- crowd rhythm;
- hydration and food gaps;
- counterfeit packaging;
- worker fatigue.

## Shift Arc

Receive and verify stock, prepare station, serve and pace, refuse or redirect service, coordinate water and food, preserve samples after an incident, reconcile inventory, clean and document.

## Mastery Styles

- sensory craft;
- high-speed service;
- harm-reduction practice;
- ritual or cultural knowledge;
- zero-intoxicant hospitality;
- supply-chain auditing.

# 3. Performer

## Core Fantasy

Build attention, emotion, rhythm, spectacle, and collective memory through a body, instrument, avatar, machine, or environment.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- room acoustics;
- audience attention and fatigue;
- stage heat and footing;
- timing with crew and automation;
- costume or body-interface strain;
- political symbols in the audience;
- moments when silence matters more than continuation.

## Shift and Project

Rehearsal, equipment and costume care, sound check, contract negotiation, performance, interruptions, encore decision, teardown, payment, emotional decompression, later public interpretation.

## Mastery Styles

- improvisational crowd reading;
- exact ensemble discipline;
- intimate small-room work;
- political performance;
- accessible multisensory design;
- technically hazardous spectacle.

# 4. Adult-Service Worker

## Core Fantasy

Provide a chosen adult service through skilled boundary-setting, social reading, performance, privacy, and worker-controlled practice.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- capacity and communication;
- client respect for boundaries;
- surveillance and privacy conditions;
- schedule pressure;
- room safety;
- body and emotional fatigue;
- coercion indicators among coworkers;
- payment irregularity.

## Shift Arc

Set availability and boundaries, prepare space and body, screen and accept or refuse clients, perform only agreed service, stop when needed, recover, document incidents privately, reconcile pay, travel safely.

## Mastery Styles

- independent practice;
- cooperative governance;
- performance-centered work;
- companionship and communication;
- accessibility specialization;
- worker advocacy.

## Hard Boundary

Mastery never increases the player’s entitlement to another person. This packet concerns the profession’s work, not explicit simulation.

# 5. Nightlife Medic

## Core Fantasy

Recognize, stabilize, protect privacy, and coordinate care amid uncertainty, crowd pressure, intoxication, and venue politics.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- breathing, skin temperature, pupil and coordination changes;
- environmental heat and crowd density;
- medication and product interactions;
- panic versus respiratory or cardiovascular danger;
- signs of coercion or assault;
- venue attempts to conceal evidence.

## Shift Arc

Inventory and calibration, briefing on circulating products, routine consultations, simultaneous incidents, transport decisions, batch alert, worker check-in, records, licensing or public-health testimony.

## Mastery Styles

- emergency stabilization;
- harm-reduction trust;
- reproductive and sexual health;
- alien or modified-body physiology;
- public-health investigation;
- peer training.

# 6. De-escalation and Venue Safety Specialist

## Core Fantasy

Keep people safe through presence, communication, positioning, boundaries, and proportional intervention rather than domination.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- crowd compression;
- gaze, posture, and movement;
- blocked exits;
- staff fear;
- escalating humiliation;
- discriminatory targeting;
- concealed weapons or tools;
- when one’s own uniform is increasing danger.

## Shift Arc

Walkthrough, team roles, accessibility and exit check, greeting and observation, intervention, medical or worker handoff, evidence preservation, use-of-force review, debrief.

## Mastery Styles

- verbal de-escalation;
- crowd flow;
- consent-support practice;
- trauma-aware intervention;
- anti-corruption work;
- high-risk extraction.

# 7. Sanitation and Recovery Worker

## Core Fantasy

Restore the city after spectacle while reading contamination, infrastructure, evidence, private property, and hidden institutional truth.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- waste streams;
- odors and chemical reactions;
- drain and ventilation behavior;
- sharps or hazardous residue;
- lost property;
- unusual cleanup requests;
- falsified disposal categories;
- evidence that should not be casually destroyed.

## Shift Arc

Protective preparation, route assignment, venue handover, sorting and containment, cleaning, infrastructure observation, private-property recovery, evidence decision, waste custody, tool decontamination, report.

## Mastery Styles

- hazardous cleanup;
- water and waste systems;
- evidence-sensitive recovery;
- high-speed event turnover;
- worker safety organizing;
- circular-material salvage.

## Public Meaning

This profession reveals whether the city truly values the people who make pleasure possible after the visitors leave.

# 8. Hospitality Worker

## Core Fantasy

Coordinate shelter, food, discretion, accessibility, cultural expectation, and crisis response for people temporarily away from home.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- room readiness;
- fatigue and distress;
- conflicting privacy needs;
- luggage and custody irregularities;
- accessibility mismatch;
- household or group tension;
- signs that a guest cannot safely return elsewhere.

## Shift Arc

Arrivals, room and identity assignment, problems and special needs, service coordination, missing guest, emergency shelter conversion, checkout, lost property, housekeeping handover, account dispute.

## Mastery Styles

- discretion;
- high-volume coordination;
- accessible hospitality;
- refugee and emergency shelter;
- cultural mediation;
- luxury expectation management.

# 9. Gambling Auditor

## Core Fantasy

Prove whether games, custody, reserves, advertising, and operator behavior remain fair under pressure from powerful people.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- statistical anomalies;
- rule-version mismatch;
- reserve shortfall;
- custody gaps;
- unusual staff access;
- advertising claims;
- suspicious player exclusion;
- apparently clean records that are too clean.

## Project Arc

Scope audit, preserve snapshots, inspect devices and procedures, interview workers, reproduce outcomes, trace reserves and payouts, issue preliminary findings, withstand pressure, publish or refer, verify correction.

## Mastery Styles

- device integrity;
- financial custody;
- worker testimony;
- statistical analysis;
- public communication;
- anti-corruption investigation.

# 10. Licensing Inspector

## Core Fantasy

Judge whether a venue may operate, under what conditions, and at whose risk—without confusing paperwork with safety or discretion with corruption.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- site flow and exits;
- staffing and credential gaps;
- paper reality versus actual practice;
- worker behavior under management presence;
- hidden spaces;
- emergency capacity;
- neighborhood burden;
- political pressure.

## Shift and Project

Complaint review, authority and conflict check, unannounced or scheduled visit, evidence, interviews, conditional order, appeal, follow-up, public hearing.

## Mastery Styles

- technical safety;
- worker-centered inspection;
- public-health practice;
- financial and ownership disclosure;
- accessibility;
- reform of obsolete rules.

# 11. Maintenance Technician

## Core Fantasy

Keep a high-load, heavily modified district functioning across power, cooling, sound, water, stages, lifts, privacy systems, and emergency infrastructure.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- vibration and harmonic change;
- heat and load patterns;
- temporary repairs hidden behind décor;
- incompatible vendor parts;
- fluid, pressure, and electrical symptoms;
- show schedules and shutdown windows;
- occupants depending on the system.

## Shift Arc

Review handover, prioritize faults, negotiate downtime, isolate safely, diagnose, source or fabricate parts, repair, test under event load, document temporary work, brief next shift.

## Mastery Styles

- legacy venues;
- live-show emergency repair;
- public utility integration;
- low-resource fabrication;
- privacy and security systems;
- preventive maintenance leadership.

# 12. Late-Shift Transit Operator

## Core Fantasy

Move workers, visitors, patients, and vulnerable people safely through peaks, closures, harassment, fatigue, and city politics.

## Sensory Vocabulary

- passenger flow;
- platform crowding;
- road or rail condition;
- intoxication and medical concern;
- route conflict;
- accessibility boarding;
- dangerous waiting areas;
- vehicle behavior and fatigue.

## Shift Arc

Vehicle and route check, worker departure peak, event surges, medical priority, service disruption, stranded passengers, crowd dispersal, depot return, lost property, handover.

## Mastery Styles

- crowd operations;
- accessible transit;
- emergency evacuation;
- route improvisation;
- conflict de-escalation;
- vehicle maintenance literacy.

# Cross-Profession Dependencies

The professions form a causal network:

```text
substance steward → medic → batch tester → regulator
performer → stage technician → sanitation → transit
adult-service worker → de-escalation → private clinic → legal support
dealer → auditor → licensing inspector → public hearing
hospitality → transport → missing-person response → archive custody
```

A functional city depends on handovers across professions, not one hero worker.

# Ordinary Monday

The benchmark must show these workers outside crisis:

- eating after shift;
- arguing about schedules;
- teaching apprentices;
- repairing uniforms and tools;
- attending school meetings;
- watching bad television;
- caring for households;
- being bored;
- considering different work;
- remembering what the visitors never saw.
