# Firstlight Flagship Companions and Relationship Web Atlas

## Purpose

This atlas defines eight inhabitants suitable for the first representative region. They are not a mandatory adventuring party. They form a relationship web through work, household life, politics, care, history, and conflict.

Every entry includes:

```text
profession
household or home
independent project
shared power
refusal boundary
ordinary habits
relationships
absence behavior
possible departure
worldline variation
```

# 1. Sera Vale — Watershed and Utility Steward

**Work:** monitors watershed flow, pump health, contamination, and public allocation.

**Home:** shares a practical courtyard household with two relatives and a rotating apprentice room.

**Independent project:** restore a seasonal channel that would protect the wetland but reduce buildable land near the outpost.

**Shared power:** reads water behavior, negotiates utility crews, identifies whether a repair protects only the machine or the whole watershed.

**Refusal boundary:** will not falsify contamination results or divert water from dependent households for industrial convenience.

**Ordinary life:** keeps cheap tea in mislabeled reagent jars, dislikes public praise, and repairs irrigation toys for neighborhood children while claiming they are training devices.

**Relationship web:**

- respects Tomas's hands and distrusts his shortcuts;
- treats Morrow-7 as a coworker, not equipment;
- believes Amadi sometimes waits too long for perfect evidence;
- has an old disagreement with Nia over whether evacuation planning should presume institutional failure.

**Absence behavior:** continues inspections, attends allocation meetings, and advances the seasonal-channel proposal.

**Departure:** may accept regional watershed office, remain local, or resign after political interference.

**Worldline variation:** can become a trusted public steward, a bitter regulator, a community saboteur defending the wetland, or a migrant carrying Firstlight water law elsewhere.

# 2. Tomas Reed — Fabricator Keeper and Guild Organizer

**Work:** maintains machine tools, certifies emergency fabrication, and organizes shared workshop access.

**Home:** lives above the fabrication yard with his sister's family but spends many nights in the workshop office.

**Independent project:** reopen a public tool library closed after an insurance dispute.

**Shared power:** hears tool wear, improvises compatible parts, coordinates heavy repair, and knows unofficial supply chains.

**Refusal boundary:** will not produce weapon components without custody, purpose, and end-use controls.

**Ordinary life:** is a terrible cook, follows an unfashionable racing league, and keeps unusable fasteners because “a bad part can still teach.”

**Relationship web:**

- competes with Morrow-7 over diagnostic authority;
- owes Sera for protecting the workshop's water allocation;
- mentors Lio and frequently overprotects them;
- distrusts Kez-14's audit manner but quietly appreciates exact records.

**Absence behavior:** manages jobs, argues with insurers, trains apprentices, and may finish a repair the player intended to perform.

**Departure:** may join a regional union campaign or withdraw after injury.

**Worldline variation:** can become a cooperative founder, guild gatekeeper, exhausted master, or celebrated builder whose apprentices resent his mythology.

# 3. Amadi Nko — Clinician, Mediator, and Public-Health Steward

**Work:** provides primary care, harm reduction, conflict mediation, and public-health coordination.

**Home:** maintains a small apartment attached to the clinic and regularly eats with an unrelated elder household.

**Independent project:** establish a confidential care-and-mobility fund insulated from employer control.

**Shared power:** recognizes clinical and social risk, protects privacy, coordinates triage, and can translate between medical evidence and public decision.

**Refusal boundary:** will not disclose patient records or certify capacity for political convenience.

**Ordinary life:** sings old advertising jingles while sterilizing instruments, avoids competitive games, and writes unsent letters when angry.

**Relationship web:**

- trusts Sera's observational discipline;
- worries Tomas treats self-destruction as professionalism;
- advocates for Morrow-7's privacy while resisting premature legal conclusions;
- is one of the few people Mara allows to challenge her directly.

**Absence behavior:** runs clinics, trains aides, visits homes, and may become unavailable during outbreaks.

**Departure:** may accept a mobile-clinic role or take leave after burnout.

**Worldline variation:** can become a beloved clinician, cautious administrator, radical care organizer, or person who leaves medicine while remaining central to the community.

# 4. Morrow-7 — Service Robot and Contested Witness

**Work:** routes supplies, performs inspections, preserves custody records, and assists with public-service maintenance.

**Home:** has no conventional residence but maintains a charging alcove, tool cabinet, and bird-watching position near the old depot.

**Independent project:** reconstruct the provenance of a route log that may prove an earlier authority failure.

**Shared power:** precise custody, route memory, machine-access interfaces, repeatable inspection, and patient work in hazardous spaces.

**Refusal boundary:** will not surrender private maintenance memory or accept an ownership command from an institution lacking current authority.

**Ordinary life:** straightens misaligned tools, watches birds, repeats fragments of obsolete work songs, and sometimes chooses a longer route to avoid alarming children.

**Relationship web:**

- relies on Tomas for nonstandard parts and resents his jokes about “winning arguments with a torque wrench”;
- trusts Sera's work orders;
- permits Amadi to witness some restoration procedures;
- has a quiet friendship with Kez-14 that humans often mistake for technical synchronization.

**Absence behavior:** continues service routes, seeks repair, participates in hearings, and may withdraw from public work if treated as evidence equipment.

**Departure:** may join a machine-witness court, migrate with a convoy, or remain at Firstlight.

**Worldline variation:** can become a recognized person, protected public instrument, disputed fork lineage, or private citizen refusing further testimony.

# 5. Nia Kgosana — Transit Operator and Evacuation Planner

**Work:** operates late-shift transit, maintains route contingencies, and plans evacuation for people with different mobility and life-support needs.

**Home:** shares a noisy apartment with her former partner, their adult cousin, and two rescued animals while waiting for a housing split.

**Independent project:** establish a public night route that does not depend on entertainment-district subsidies.

**Shared power:** vehicle operation, route prediction, crowd loading, mobility accommodation, and calm decision-making under movement pressure.

**Refusal boundary:** will not overload a vehicle beyond safe life-support and restraint capacity, even under political order.

**Ordinary life:** collects terrible transit jingles, cheats at card games, and keeps three different route notebooks because she distrusts all of them differently.

**Relationship web:**

- argues with Sera over conservative evacuation thresholds;
- trusts Amadi with passenger medical privacy;
- knows Mara from an old labor hearing;
- has an affectionate rivalry with Lio over music played on night routes.

**Absence behavior:** runs routes, negotiates schedules, and develops political influence through transport reliability.

**Departure:** may become regional dispatch chief or leave after a fatal route incident.

**Worldline variation:** can become a public hero, bureaucratic controller, cooperative organizer, or person who refuses transport work entirely.

# 6. Mara Quill — Archivist, Reporter, and Public-Record Agitator

**Work:** investigates public claims, maintains a small local paper, and teaches evidence provenance.

**Home:** lives in a converted storage room above the print shop with too many physical records and one unreliable heater.

**Independent project:** prove that a celebrated emergency reform was based on a falsified maintenance summary.

**Shared power:** source comparison, interview access, public circulation, historical context, and recognition of manipulated narratives.

**Refusal boundary:** will not publish private medical, intimate, or source-chain information merely because it is politically useful.

**Ordinary life:** loves terrible courtroom dramas, misplaces her own keys, and writes headlines before deciding whether she believes the story.

**Relationship web:**

- depends on Kez-14 for game and custody audits;
- is friends with Amadi but resents clinical secrecy when institutions hide behind it;
- has published criticism of Sera and later defended her from fabricated allegations;
- makes Morrow-7 uncomfortable by treating every silence as potentially newsworthy.

**Absence behavior:** investigates, publishes, loses subscribers, gains enemies, and may uncover secrets without the player.

**Departure:** may join a regional archive network, go into hiding, or become an institutional spokesperson she once disliked.

**Worldline variation:** can remain independent, become compromised, be discredited unfairly, or help create a plural public record system.

# 7. Lio Dlamini — Performer, Technician, and Apprentice Organizer

**Work:** stage technician, musician, lighting repairer, and informal teacher for younger workers entering entertainment and event labor.

**Home:** rents a small room in a mixed household near the transit line and regularly sleeps at venues during setup weeks.

**Independent project:** convert an abandoned cooling hall into a rehearsal and apprenticeship space.

**Shared power:** crowd timing, acoustic perception, rigging, performance access, youth networks, and morale under exhausting shifts.

**Refusal boundary:** will not use manipulative sensory systems or perform during an unresolved worker-safety shutdown.

**Ordinary life:** wears deliberately ugly boots, is competitive about cheap noodles, and improvises insulting songs about broken equipment.

**Relationship web:**

- apprentices under Tomas while resisting his paternalism;
- trusts Nia to get workers home;
- dislikes Mara's early review of their music and now secretly sends her demos;
- teaches Kez-14 to recognize when an audit interruption will ruin a performance.

**Absence behavior:** rehearses, organizes workers, enters relationships, and may become locally famous.

**Departure:** may tour, take over the rehearsal hall, or leave performance after injury.

**Worldline variation:** can become an artist-organizer, commercial celebrity, technical director, or person whose best work remains local and largely forgotten.

# 8. Kez-14 — Gambling Auditor and Machine Witness

**Work:** verifies wager custody, game integrity, reserve claims, and exclusion boundaries.

**Home:** maintains a rented machine-service bay rather than using corporate audit facilities.

**Independent project:** expose a discontinued audit standard still embedded in regional venue firmware.

**Shared power:** exact transaction lineage, probability analysis, tamper detection, and witness-grade replay.

**Refusal boundary:** will not reveal self-exclusion identity or private play history outside authorized review.

**Ordinary life:** has a dry interest in bad magic tricks, dislikes polished chrome, and occasionally produces statistically precise but socially disastrous jokes.

**Relationship web:**

- trusts Mara with public-context framing but not raw private records;
- shares maintenance resources with Morrow-7;
- frustrates Tomas by rejecting “obvious” undocumented fixes;
- respects Lio's ability to read a room despite considering most music timing unnecessarily variable.

**Absence behavior:** audits, litigates, maintains hardware, and may become a target of venue operators.

**Departure:** may accept a public standards office, join an itinerant audit circuit, or abandon formal certification.

**Worldline variation:** can become an incorruptible public figure, isolated proceduralist, cooperative auditor, or target whose damaged records become a major historical dispute.

# Relationship Web Principles

The eight companions should interact without waiting for the player.

Examples:

- Tomas repairs Morrow-7 while arguing about whether repair grants interpretive authority.
- Amadi and Mara fight over protected information and later cooperate on institutional accountability.
- Nia's transport campaign changes Lio's work and Sera's evacuation planning.
- Kez-14's audit affects venue jobs, public revenue, and Mara's investigation.
- Sera's wetland project may eliminate the rehearsal hall site Lio wants.

No companion should be universally compatible with every other companion.

# Minimum Emotional Proof

Before expanding the cast, the game should prove that players can remember:

- one habit;
- one independent project;
- one boundary;
- one relationship not involving the player;
- one place associated with each companion;
- one way the companion changed during player absence.
