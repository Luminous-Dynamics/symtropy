# Historical Texture Timeline and Relationship Map

## Purpose

This map binds named lore into the existing macro-history without pretending every worldline shares one exact chronology.

Dates are reference anchors. Procedural history may shift them while preserving causal ordering and ancestry.

# Worldline Transformation Rules

A worldline may:

- prevent an event;
- reduce or intensify it;
- change the responsible institution;
- preserve different evidence;
- alter who becomes famous;
- transform a corporation into a commons or state earlier;
- leave a city destroyed, flourishing, occupied, migrated, or never founded;
- produce different artistic and diaspora responses.

It must not generate contradictory ancestry without a recorded fork, migration, forgery, or archival uncertainty.

Historical texture is strongest when the player can trace a present door, song, law, scar, recipe, or argument back through several linked causes.
