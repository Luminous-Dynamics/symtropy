# 1. Core Regional Thesis

The Southern African Water-Energy Compact is about water scarcity becoming constitutional memory.

The region did not survive by pretending water was abundant. It survived by making water movement visible, lawful, witnessed, repairable, and politically expensive to capture.

Its strongest settlements combine:

```text
solar abundance
water austerity
repair literacy
analog anti-Null infrastructure
basin-scale law
community hydro-rights
mining remediation
convoy logistics
public witness culture
```

Design rule:

```text
Every water pipe should also be a legal argument.
```

The player should never see water as a simple resource meter here. Water is:

```text
life-support
memory
land claim
community right
energy constraint
repair obligation
political inheritance
possible weapon
```

## Pressure Outsiders Misunderstand

Outsiders often assume the region is simply water-poor.

Locals understand the deeper issue:

```text
Water exists in cycles, claims, pipes, aquifers, courts, memory, labor, and trust.
Scarcity is not only rainfall.
Scarcity is broken legitimacy over flow.
```

## Pressure Locals Consider Sacred

```text
No hidden water law.
No private valve without public witness.
No emergency diversion without expiration.
No basin authority without appeal.
```

# 6. Mechanical Modifiers

```yaml
mechanical_modifiers:
  water_logic:
    - water access is basin-mediated, not purely local
    - major valves require hydro-rights proof, acoustic calibration, or public witness
    - emergency diversions create legitimacy debt unless recorded and expired
  power_logic:
    - solar energy is abundant during clear days but water limits cooling, cleaning, and manufacturing
    - dust and grit reduce panel efficiency without maintenance
    - battery reserves become political during drought nights
  food_logic:
    - water-efficient agriculture and greenhouse rationing dominate settlement planning
    - restoration wetlands compete with food plots for allocation
  travel_logic:
    - convoy routes are trusted or distrusted based on route memory and water debt
    - remote travel requires water-credit planning, not only fuel
  access_logic:
    - recognized basin citizens move easily through court gates
    - refugees may receive temporary care tokens but not full water voting rights
    - informal settlements often have practical access but weak legal access
  construction_logic:
    - high-water infrastructure requires public registration
    - unregistered boreholes trigger civic conflict
    - major fabrication nodes need water-cooling permits
  device_bus_logic:
    - old digital water systems are low-trust
    - high-authority hydro-controls require analog acoustic verification
    - wireless commands may be ignored by Lithic Valve Custodians
  field_deck_logic:
    - CIVIC mode foregrounds water legitimacy
    - DIAG mode links pump state to basin allocation
    - ARCHIVE mode surfaces old claims and mining liability
    - NULL mode watches for obsolete contract enforcement and automated denial loops
  social_logic:
    - public trust rises when repair is witnessed
    - legitimacy falls sharply after hidden diversions
    - guild certification affects repair speed and NPC confidence
  ecology_logic:
    - wetlands reduce toxin load but consume protected water allocation
    - mine-scar remediation changes faction trust and food-web recovery
  combat_logic:
    - firefights near acoustic gates can trigger valve misalignment or court lockdown

  chronicle_logic:
    - water decisions are high-significance events
```
