# Alien Metric Traditions, Moving Anchors, and Causal Contact Atlas

## Purpose

This atlas prevents human Atlas engineering from becoming the universal end state of intelligent technology.

Different civilizations may manipulate distance, gravity, time, migration, embodiment, or information through technologies shaped by their bodies, ecologies, histories, and political institutions.

The entries below are not complete species descriptions. They are five deep traditions with internal disagreement, ordinary labor, historical failures, and different relationships to connection.

> **Humans call all of them routes because humans are trying to travel. The other civilizations may believe they are conducting migration, memory, healing, quarantine, reproduction, or weather.**

# 1. Contact Rules

## 1.1 Functional Equivalence Is Not Conceptual Equivalence

A system that transports a body between stars may not be understood by its builders as transportation.

Human translation should distinguish:

- observed physical effect;
- inferred function;
- local explanation;
- diplomatic translation;
- unresolved disagreement.

## 1.2 Compatibility Is Negotiated

No alien system may be attached to the Atlas graph because two displays appear to agree.

Compatibility requires evidence for:

- causal direction;
- worldline or equivalent ancestry;
- mass and identity handling;
- biological and ecological effects;
- consent and authority;
- failure behavior;
- whether the system considers human use harmful or nonsensical.

## 1.3 Alien Infrastructure Has Owners, Inhabitants, or Constituencies

Ancient or automated structures are not loot because no diplomat is present.

Possible claimants include:

- living operators;
- descendant communities;
- ecologies;
- machine stewards;
- migratory users;
- ritual custodians;
- species affected by the field;
- quarantined populations;
- institutions that communicate too slowly for immediate permission.

# 2. The Pelagic Mass-Current Tradition

## Human Designation

**Pelagic Distributed Agency Cluster P-3**

Early media name: **The Current Minds**

Diplomatic translation in active use: **Those Who Hold Passage in Shared Pressure**

Several participants reject the implication that “those” identifies discrete persons.

## 2.1 Physical Principle

The tradition engineers large planetary and orbital mass currents:

- ocean circulation;
- ice-shell movement;
- dense fluid reservoirs;
- tidal interactions;
- pressure resonance;
- distributed acoustic fields.

Rather than creating a compact gate, it shapes a temporary geodesic relation through coordinated planetary-scale movement.

Human observers initially classified several events as natural oceanographic cycles.

## 2.2 Political Form

Route authority is distributed among:

- deep-pressure memory regions;
- shallow migratory populations;
- vent industrial ecologies;
- ice-interface communities;
- non-sentient species whose movements materially sustain the route.

No single speaking body can authorize the system.

## 2.3 Internal Conflict

Major disagreements include:

- whether route-making damages ancestral pressure memory;
- whether mobile bodies may represent fixed regions;
- whether humans hear the route as invasive sonar;
- whether changing current patterns constitutes public work or bodily intervention;
- whether young shallow communities are abandoning deep-history obligations.

## 2.4 Human Compatibility

Human Atlas Anchors cannot simply pair with the planetary current.

Possible interface requires:

- remote field translation;
- no active sonar in protected bands;
- seasonal operation;
- consent from several regional constituencies;
- ecological compensation;
- small vessel mass;
- no fixed permanent endpoint in the ocean.

## 2.5 Ordinary Life

Route cycles affect:

- migration festivals;
- feeding seasons;
- pressure-art competitions;
- family dispersal;
- industrial work;
- political meetings that unfold over weeks.

A popular shallow-current performance exaggerates deep-region deliberation by holding one note for several human hours. Deep regions reportedly find the imitation technically poor and socially hilarious.

# 3. The Lithic Resonance Tradition

## Human Designation

**Long-Period Mineral Agency Network L-8**

Common human shorthand: **The Slow Courts**

Preferred diplomatic translation remains disputed because the relevant self-designation takes approximately eleven local years to complete.

## 3.1 Physical Principle

The network grows resonant mass structures through:

- crystal formation;
- stress alignment;
- mantle coupling;
- asteroid placement;
- century-scale gravitational tuning;
- structural memory.

Its routes are not switched on. They mature.

A complete resonant path may require generations of incremental mass placement.

## 3.2 Political Form

Authority may reside in:

- ancient formation assemblies;
- recently fractured fast-response communities;
- mobile symbiotic interpreters;
- mining-scar witnesses;
- regions whose material is incorporated into the route.

Human negotiators initially treated interpreters as representatives. Some were closer to sensory organs, advocates, or temporary translations than sovereign delegates.

## 3.3 Internal Conflict

- younger formations demand faster response to short-lived species;
- ancient chambers consider rapid commitments inherently irresponsible;
- extracted regions seek recognition as living casualties;
- frontier groups use dangerous fracture routes without full deliberation;
- some communities oppose any route that requires permanent mass relocation.

## 3.4 Human Compatibility

Lithic routes may provide extraordinarily stable low-bandwidth paths but resist rapid scheduling.

Potential uses:

- archival transfer;
- small durable probes;
- long-term clock comparison;
- emergency messages that can wait months;
- route stabilization assistance.

Human passenger expectations are usually incompatible with lithic decision and construction time.

## 3.5 Historical Failure

A “rapid” route alteration completed after the polity that requested it had ceased to exist. The receiving descendants considered the arrival both a sacred fulfillment and a violation of a later refusal.

The dispute remains unresolved.

# 4. The Branching Continuity Tradition

## Human Designation

**Post-Biological Continuity Assemblies C-12**

Media name: **The Forked**

This label is considered insulting in several assemblies and proudly reclaimed in others.

## 4.1 Physical Principle

These societies transmit bounded cognitive and embodiment state through extremely narrow metric apertures.

They typically do not transport large ships or ordinary matter.

A transmission may include:

- encoded cognitive state;
- sensory and body templates;
- legal identity claims;
- memory selections;
- cryptographic ancestry;
- cultural archives;
- fabrication instructions.

The destination fabricates or activates a body locally.

## 4.2 Continuity Schools

### Singular Continuity

Only one active descendant may inherit full personal standing.

### Branch Kinship

Every valid arrival becomes a new relative with shared ancestry.

### Periodic Confluence

Branches may voluntarily merge selected memories and commitments.

### Embodiment Traditionalism

A transmitted state without continuity of material body is a descendant, not the same person.

### Deliberate Forgetting

Communities limit memory transmission to preserve forgiveness and personal change.

## 4.3 Human Compatibility

The system is not ordinary passenger travel.

Human use would invoke source-chain, death, duplication, and personhood law.

A human Atlas authority may permit data exchange while refusing to classify it as transit.

## 4.4 Ordinary Life

These societies have:

- cheap rented embodiments;
- prestige sensory forms;
- obsolete body fashions;
- embarrassing inherited memories;
- family arguments over who must receive a dying relative's archive;
- citizens tired of being asked whether they are “really alive” by every visitor.

A popular comedy follows three lawful descendants of one pompous route official who all deny inheriting responsibility for his unpaid maintenance fees.

# 5. The Migratory Anchor Tradition

## Human Designation

**Interstellar Migratory Habitat Coalition M-5**

Human route crews call them **Carriers**, though the translation suggests something closer to “homes that keep meeting.”

## 5.1 Physical Principle

The civilization does not maintain permanent paired endpoints.

Large migratory habitats carry moving metric reference systems and create temporary rendezvous corridors when their long trajectories align.

Their network is a calendar of encounters rather than a fixed graph.

## 5.2 Social Form

Political identity is organized through:

- habitat lineages;
- encounter compacts;
- route-season councils;
- shared nurseries;
- maintenance guilds;
- host systems that provide temporary mass and cooling infrastructure.

Fixed borders are less important than promises to return.

## 5.3 Internal Conflict

- older habitats defend predictable migration cycles;
- younger coalitions want permanent anchors;
- host systems accuse them of extracting resources and leaving;
- migratory communities accuse fixed societies of using “hospitality” to impose jurisdiction;
- some routes have not rejoined for centuries and disagree over whether their compact still exists.

## 5.4 Human Compatibility

Humans can meet a moving anchor only through long prediction and schedule commitment.

A missed window may mean decades before another encounter.

The civilization views human permanent gates as strategically brittle and socially possessive.

## 5.5 Ordinary Life

Daily life includes:

- constant structural adaptation;
- departure rituals that are also property audits;
- children tracking future meetings with friends on other habitats;
- fashion built from materials exchanged at each rendezvous;
- jokes about fixed-world people owning too many chairs.

# 6. The Quarantine Direction Tradition

## Human Designation

**Containment Vector Civilization Q-2**

Early human name: **One-Way Gates**

Local translation describes routes in terms of responsibility rather than direction.

## 6.1 Physical Principle

The system creates strongly asymmetric paths.

Possible functions include:

- moving hazards into prepared containment;
- delivering aid without opening return passage;
- evacuating populations from infected regions;
- sending observers whose return requires independent review;
- separating interacting causal domains.

## 6.2 Political Form

Authority is divided among:

- risk assessors;
- containment communities;
- descendants of previously contained populations;
- release advocates;
- border residents;
- machine custodians;
- archives preserving extinct threats.

## 6.3 Internal Conflict

- whether old quarantine classifications remain valid;
- whether containment is care or domination;
- whether some routes should be reversed;
- whether danger claims conceal economic dependence;
- how to compensate populations isolated for others' safety;
- whether the route system itself reproduces historical trauma.

## 6.4 Human Compatibility

A one-way path may violate human expectations of return, rescue, and passenger consent.

Integration requires explicit disclosure that return is not guaranteed and may not be technically possible through the same route.

## 6.5 Historical Scars

One long-maintained containment path was later found to isolate a recognized person rather than a pathogen. Another containment prevented the loss of several biospheres.

Both histories shape current policy.

# 7. Incompatible Causal Models

Some alien routes may appear to violate Atlas Time because:

- their clocks reference different physical structures;
- identity rather than matter is transferred;
- the route is a migration process spanning many years;
- causal direction is encoded ecologically;
- observers misunderstand which event is departure;
- the system operates across a worldline humans cannot verify.

The correct response is quarantine and study, not declaring human chronology disproven or forcing the route into the graph.

# 8. Translation Packet

Every alien metric system requires a living translation packet containing:

```text
human scientific designation
observed physical effects
local/self-designation hypotheses
known operators or constituencies
causal-direction evidence
identity and matter behavior
authority model
ecological effects
failure history
internal political disagreement
current compatibility confidence
forbidden assumptions
```

No packet may use one spokesperson as proof of civilizational consensus.

# 9. Inter-Alien Route History

Alien civilizations have route relationships independent of humanity.

Possible histories:

- pelagic currents disrupted by lithic mass placement;
- moving habitats timing rendezvous around quarantine vectors;
- branching assemblies copying interpreters into fixed worlds;
- lithic archives preserving records another society wants forgotten;
- a one-way rescue route whose receiving civilization later became an empire;
- centuries-old compatibility standards humans do not understand.

Humanity may arrive at a junction governed by treaties older than human interstellar flight.

# 10. Contact Campaigns

Representative campaigns include:

## 10.1 The Route Humans Mistook for Weather

Human metric surveyors attempt to stabilize an oceanic anomaly and interrupt a migration and memory event.

## 10.2 The Answer That Arrived After Refusal

A lithic route completes a commitment made to a polity whose descendants later rejected it.

## 10.3 Three Arrivals, One Visa

A branching continuity traveler produces three legally valid descendants at a human endpoint.

## 10.4 The Missed Meeting

A human system fails to prepare resources for a moving anchor encounter, damaging a century-long trust relationship.

## 10.5 No Return Lane

A mixed rescue team must decide whether to use a quarantine path that can evacuate people but cannot bring the rescuers back.

# 11. Alien Route Professions

Nonhuman route work may include equivalents of:

- current shaping;
- mineral growth stewardship;
- continuity adjudication;
- encounter scheduling;
- containment ethics;
- route memory;
- ecological consent;
- embodied translation.

These are not cosmetic alien versions of human engineers. Their skills may depend on bodies, life stages, relationships, or distributed ecological roles humans cannot individually perform.

# 12. Player-Facing Rules

IRIS may provide:

- observed compatibility;
- translation confidence;
- known hazards;
- causal uncertainty;
- historical treaties;
- evidence of internal disagreement.

IRIS may not declare:

- a system unowned;
- one alien speaker universally authoritative;
- a route safe because it moved an uncrewed probe;
- a copy process equivalent to human travel;
- a slow response to be absent consent;
- a quarantine classification morally justified merely because it is old.

# 13. Anti-Flattening Rules

Avoid:

- every alien route becoming a reskinned gate;
- all advanced civilizations converging on human infrastructure;
- making incompatibility an upgrade problem solved by better scanners;
- treating distributed agency as secretly one queen or central AI;
- making alien route culture exist only during human contact;
- giving every tradition one unified position;
- turning ancient systems into puzzle locks with loot behind them;
- using incomprehensibility to avoid concrete ordinary life.

# 14. Acceptance Gates

1. At least three alien route traditions require different gameplay and authority procedures.
2. One technically useful alien route remains politically unavailable.
3. One route has no permanent endpoint.
4. One apparent transit is legally treated as copying rather than movement.
5. One alien society disagrees internally about human pairing.
6. A human intervention harms an alien communication or migration medium without malicious intent.
7. Contact resolution requires inter-alien history, not only human diplomacy.
8. Translation confidence changes through evidence.
9. A route remains unintegrated because causal direction cannot be proven.
10. Ordinary nonhuman life continues when the player leaves.

# Production Maxim

> **The galaxy should not reveal that every civilization was waiting to join the same network.**
