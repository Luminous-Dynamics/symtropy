# Symtropy Vehicle & Mobility Design Bible

## Version 0.1 — Mobility as Civilization

## Core Thesis

Vehicles in Symtropy are not just transportation.

They are mobile infrastructure, political objects, repair platforms, care systems, legal jurisdictions, memory carriers, and visible promises.

A vehicle should answer:

```text
What does it carry?
Who controls it?
Who maintains it?
Who is allowed aboard?
What infrastructure does it depend on?
What society does it make possible?
What failure mode does it create?
```

Symtropy vehicles are not mainly about speed, luxury, domination, or spectacle.

They are about:

```text
water
repair
rescue
witness
care
trade
movement
memory
ecology
law
survival
public trust
```

A truck is not just a truck.

It may be:

```text
a public water promise
a mobile clinic
a contested charter asset
a rescue debt
a machine testimony carrier
a faction symbol
a repair school
a law dispute on wheels
```

A spacecraft is not just a ship.

It may be:

```text
a pressure-vessel constitution
a water ledger
a rescue jurisdiction
an archive vault
a seed monastery
a closed-loop society
a machine ecology
```

# Global Design Rules

## 1. Vehicles should not trivialize the world

Travel must remain meaningful.

Vehicles should help players cross distance, but they should not erase:

```text
route danger
weather
maintenance
fuel
crew fatigue
social permission
faction control
repair debt
public legitimacy
```

## 2. Mobility is political

Every vehicle has a civic status:

```text
public trust
guild maintained
commons owned
charter protected
company restricted
emergency requisitioned
unregistered
machine stewarded
archive certified
refugee compact protected
salvage disputed
ghost authority controlled
```

## 3. Vehicles accumulate history

Vehicles should not only have hit points.

They should gain story marks:

```text
patched axle
old rescue debt
Archive-certified override
public trust seal
Null exposure tag
unauthorized firmware
crew memorial ribbon
worker repair marks
contaminated water tank
recovered archive scratches
storm damage
family tool symbol
charter dispute tag
```

## 4. Maintenance is gameplay

Every vehicle depends on:

```text
fuel or charge
water/cooling
filters
tires/tracks/seals
batteries
rotors
software integrity
crew skill
repair parts
public authorization
safe routes
```

## 5. Vehicles change how origins read the world

Different origins interpret the same vehicle differently.

Examples:

```text
Worker-Guild Mechanic:
  sees maintenance lineage and poor repair work.

Archive Apprentice:
  sees ownership history and legitimacy gaps.

Field Medic:
  sees patient capacity and sanitation risk.

Refugee Charter Child:
  sees access barriers and exclusion policy.

Corporate Utility Defector:
  sees hidden locks, metering systems, and subscription logic.

Null-Touched Survivor:
  sees false green diagnostics and corrupted autonomy.

Offworld Returnee:
  sees life-support discipline or lack of it.
```

```text
salvage-rich
ecologically fragile
low-trust
high-value
dead-authority controlled
```

## Ownership / Charter Modes

Vehicles inherit politics from their owning charter.

```text
Public Trust:
  transparent logs, public access rules, strong legitimacy.

Repair Guild:
  high maintenance quality, certification restrictions.

Corporate Utility:
  reliable service, exclusion risk, metering and private locks.

Security Protectorate:
  strong crisis response, emergency drift danger.

Machine Stewardship:
  machine testimony required before override.

Refugee Compact:
  protected humanitarian use, capacity pressure.

Unregistered:
  flexible and risky, weak legal protection.

Archive Certified:
  truth-preserving, slower but more legitimate.
```

# Vehicle Design Mantra

```text
Do not ask only:
How does it move?

Ask:
Who does it save?
Who does it exclude?
Who repairs it?
Who records its use?
Who may command it?
Who pays its maintenance debt?
Who trusts it when the road fails?
```

Final thesis:

```text
In Symtropy, mobility is not escape.

Mobility is how civilization reaches the places it has almost abandoned.
```
