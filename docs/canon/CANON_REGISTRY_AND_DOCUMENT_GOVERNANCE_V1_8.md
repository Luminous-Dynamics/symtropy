# Canon Registry and Document Governance

## Purpose

The Symtropy corpus is large enough that authority must be explicit.

```text
A newer file is not automatically canonical.
A longer file is not automatically authoritative.
A concept catalog is not a production commitment.
A benchmark is not an implementation claim until evidence exists.
```

Authority boundaries:

```text
world state owns facts and consequences
NPC cognition owns bounded proposals and interpretation
relationship state owns negotiated permissions and domain trust
companions retain their bodies, offices, memories, projects, and refusal
IRIS owns assistance and evidence presentation, not private cognition
secret content owns optional discovery, never critical consent or safety truth
```

The v2.2 documents do not supersede general NPC, relationship, profession, IRIS, or persistence authorities. They specialize those systems for persistent companion relationships.

## Status Vocabulary

```text
canonical
canonical-draft
supporting
implementation-spec
experimental
historical
superseded
unclassified
```

- **canonical** defines current product truth.
- **canonical-draft** defines intended direction awaiting implementation and review evidence.
- **supporting** expands active canon without owning contradictions.
- **implementation-spec** defines data, runtime, production, or validation behavior.
- **experimental** may not silently create milestone scope.
- **historical** is retained for provenance.
- **superseded** has a named replacement.
- **unclassified** is not authoritative by default.

# Authority Boundaries

## Pleasure Is Not Consent

Economic exchange, ownership, reputation, rescue, romance, employment, or player authority never establishes consent.

## Regulation Is Not Legitimacy

Licensing and inspection produce evidence and authority state. They do not prove moral legitimacy or erase informal service value.

## Documentation Is Not Competence

Records assist professional work. They do not replace embodied practice, judgment, tools, or supervision.

## IRIS Is Not the Professional

IRIS may assist perception and records. It may not silently certify work, infer consent, diagnose from insufficient evidence, or become moral police.

## Design Is Not Implementation

All v2.1 additions remain D3 / I0 until schemas, code, replay, persistence, performance, privacy, accessibility, localization, subject review, multiplayer safety, and playtest evidence exist.

# Supersession Rules

A replacement document must:

1. declare the replaced path;
2. preserve unresolved evidence and history;
3. update incoming references;
4. state authority changes;
5. regenerate registry, link audit, quality audit, and manifest.
