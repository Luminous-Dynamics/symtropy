# Canon Registry and Document Governance

## Purpose

This document integrates the v2.6 playable-realization campaign and establishes the first forty hours of Firstlight as the current highest-priority production proof.

The broader planetary, interstellar, alien, machine, political, and worldline designs remain valid. They do not outrank evidence that the first region is physically enjoyable, emotionally memorable, accessible, replayable, and worth returning to.

# Status Vocabulary

- `canonical` — current binding design authority;
- `canonical-draft` — binding direction pending implementation evidence or final review;
- `implementation-spec` — concrete runtime or production design, not proof of code;
- `lore-canonical-draft` — approved worldbuilding direction subject to content validation;
- `supporting` — context that does not override the decision spine;
- `superseded` — retained for history but no longer current authority;
- `archived` — preserved artifact outside active design;
- `unclassified` — legacy document not yet migrated.

# Authority Order

When documents disagree, use:

1. safety, dignity, consent, rights and accessibility constraints;
2. authoritative world-state, identity, chronology and multiplayer truth;
3. current canonical contracts;
4. current implementation specifications;
5. production standards and benchmarks;
6. lore and content bibles;
7. supporting and historical documents.

# Core Boundaries

## Firstlight Is Not a Feature Tour

The opening should not showcase every major system once. It should make a smaller set of systems recur until they acquire fluency and memory.

## Love Is Not a Metric Hack

Time played, daily return, and completion are insufficient evidence.

## Action Is Not Excused by Consequence

Moral and systemic depth cannot compensate for weak movement, tools, combat, rescue or material response.

## Ordinary Life Is Not Filler

Food, music, work, rest, humor and cleanup establish what danger threatens.

## NPCs Are Not Retention Devices

Companions may not simulate distress or abandonment to compel login.

## Assistance Is Not Lesser Play

Accessibility settings change interface and execution burden, not causal legitimacy.

## Failure Does Not Require Reload

The opening must support continued play through injury, damage, incomplete evidence, public disagreement and altered routes.

## The Wider Galaxy Remains a Horizon

Atlas travel, seed ships, alien societies and worldline characters remain future possibilities. The opening reveals them without requiring implementation before Firstlight passes.

# v2.7 Firstlight Breaking and Mobile Continuity

## Opening Historical Transition

[Breaking of Firstlight Loss, Continuity, and Player Agency Contract](./BREAKING_OF_FIRSTLIGHT_LOSS_CONTINUITY_AND_PLAYER_AGENCY_CONTRACT_V0_1.md) owns the decision that Firstlight's stable civic form ends during the opening while its people, ruins, records, ecology, and disputed history persist.

The player cannot save the settlement as a whole. The player can alter:

- survivor outcomes;
- route viability;
- medical continuity;
- records and evidence;
- tools and ecological cultures;
- institutional authority;
- what enters the Continuance Crawler;
- what remains recoverable later.

The contract supersedes only the stationary-opening assumption of the first-forty-hour design. Its standards for love, physical play, ordinary life, accessibility, and honest retention remain active.

## Unique Worldline Casts

[Unique Worldline Cast Generation, Identity, and Persistence Runtime](../tech/UNIQUE_WORLDLINE_CAST_GENERATION_IDENTITY_AND_PERSISTENCE_RUNTIME_V0_1.md) owns cast seeding, role anchors, household coherence, persistent identities, multiplayer authority, and branch semantics.

[Firstlight Role Archetypes and Generated Cast Content Bible](../lore/FIRSTLIGHT_ROLE_ARCHETYPES_AND_GENERATED_CAST_CONTENT_BIBLE_V0_1.md) owns the authored role grammar.

# v2.7 Core Boundaries

- Firstlight is broken, not deleted.
- Named residents are unique to a shared worldline, not universal across all players.
- Generated people remain persistent persons after role assignment.
- The catastrophe is fixed pressure with variable causal outcome.
- Missing is not dead.
- People are not cargo or vehicle modules.
- The crawler has real mass, power, heat, water, labor, property, and route constraints.
- Operational control does not imply ownership of occupants or public assets.
- The mobile base cannot replace settlements, legitimacy, or ecological permission.
- The opening must remain replayable, accessible, and nonmanipulative.
