# System Interaction and Dependency Map

## v2.2 Causal Spine

```text
companion life anchors, professions, households, projects, and obligations
→ perceived situation and bounded cognition
→ player request, companion initiative, or shared procedure
→ scoped authority, consent, resources, competence, and risk checks
→ accept, counter, defer, challenge, or refuse
→ authoritative physical and social execution
→ outcome, handover, evidence, relationship update, and memory
→ continued household, project, career, and institutional life
→ absence, return, departure, grief, restoration, or worldline divergence
```

# 1. Authority Boundaries

## World Simulation

Owns bodies, locations, tools, resources, hazards, travel, work, and outcomes.

It does not own private relationship meaning or consent.

## NPC Cognition

Owns bounded perception, belief, intention proposals, appraisal, and initiative.

It does not own authoritative facts or guaranteed success.

## Companion Relationship State

Owns domain trust, practiced procedures, negotiated permissions, commitments, conflicts, and active relationship form.

It does not own the companion's body, office, household, memories, or general obedience.

## Profession and Institution Systems

Own competence, credentials, liability, work orders, schedules, office powers, and procedural standards.

Companion closeness cannot bypass them.

## IRIS

Owns player-facing evidence assistance, reminders, uncertainty, and shared-history retrieval within scope.

It may not reveal private companion cognition or decide consent, guilt, diagnosis, personhood, or relationship truth.

## Persistence and Worldlines

Own stable identity, source-chain roots, branch ancestry, save migration, and recovery evidence.

A restored or forked companion requires renewed relationship and authority negotiation.

## Secret Content

Owns optional discovery predicates, presentations, and rewards.

It may not hide critical safety, consent, accessibility, or tutorial information.

# 2. Joint Procedure Flow

```text
procedure selected
→ roles proposed
→ participants assess willingness and competence
→ required tools and authority reserved
→ synchronization begins
→ interruptions and challenges remain available
→ world validates each consequential action
→ evidence and handover produced
→ practiced-coordination state updated after review
```

# 3. Refusal and Counterproposal Flow

```text
request received
→ perceived facts and claimed authority checked
→ obligations, boundaries, competence, capacity, and risk evaluated
→ response selected
→ player receives grounded semantic response
→ alternative plan may be proposed
→ no action executes unless accepted and validated
```

# 4. Absence and Return Flow

```text
companion leaves active region
→ work, household, project, care, and communication packets continue
→ named identity and major obligations remain preserved across LOD
→ causal events update memory and relationship state
→ player returns
→ IRIS summarizes only legitimately available changes
→ companion presents their own interpretation when willing
```

# 5. Emotional Continuity Dependencies

Emotional investment requires at least:

- repeated places;
- ordinary routines;
- independent projects;
- relationships not centered on the player;
- remembered shared practice;
- credible disagreement;
- visible absence;
- persistent aftermath;
- private information that remains private;
- loss or change that cannot be replaced by equivalent stats.

# 6. Multiplayer

- companions are unique authoritative entities;
- no player globally owns them;
- request and delegation identity is preserved;
- conflicting requests are evaluated by the companion and world authority;
- private bonds are viewer-scoped;
- migration cannot duplicate unique companions;
- companions may prefer, trust, avoid, or refuse different players independently.

# 7. Integration Gates

Fail integration if:

- relationship state bypasses law or material reality;
- IRIS reveals hidden motives;
- companions teleport;
- off-screen simulation freezes them;
- romance gates strongest capability;
- a refusal is rendered but ignored by execution;
- a secret leaks worldline state;
- death or departure silently replaces the companion's emotional role;
- background updates invent intimate events without causal records.
