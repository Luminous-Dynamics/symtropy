# Health, Trauma, Recovery, and Care Contract

## Owned Question

**How can Symtropy make bodies, illness, disability, trauma, recovery, and care materially meaningful without turning suffering into grind, spectacle, moral judgment, or exploitative difficulty?**

## Core Thesis

Health is not a hit-point bar.

A living body is a changing relationship among:

```text
energy
pain
mobility
sensation
immune state
sleep
nutrition
stress
adaptation
care
technology
environment
social support
```

Injury and illness should affect how a person lives, not merely how efficiently they fight.

Care is not a consumable item. It is skilled labor, attention, trust, infrastructure, time, privacy, and relationship.

```text
Recovery is not returning to a universal normal.
Recovery is regaining agency, stability, meaning, or a new workable life.
```

## Prime Directives

1. **No suffering-as-content treadmill.** Harm must not be generated merely to keep care systems busy.
2. **No disability as moral failure or universal tragedy.** Disabled people can have full lives, expertise, intimacy, power, joy, and agency.
3. **No instant trauma cure.** Safety, time, relationships, body state, and meaning matter.
4. **No deterministic “madness” behavior.** Trauma never authorizes random violence or removes personhood.
5. **No private-health omniscience.** Medical state follows strict disclosure rules.
6. **No compulsory optimization.** Players and NPCs may refuse treatment, augmentation, sedation, memory alteration, or normalization within bounded safety rules.
7. **Care work is visible and valued.** Clinical, household, community, machine, ecological, and self-care all consume real capacity.
8. **Symptoms have causes and uncertainty.** Diagnosis is staged and may be wrong.
9. **Accommodations are infrastructure, not charity bonuses.** Built environments and tools should change capability.
10. **Gameplay burden must remain humane.** Repetition, pain simulation, sensory effects, and dependency mechanics require accessibility controls and friction budgets.

# 1. Health Domains

Health is multidimensional.

```text
structural integrity
blood / fluid stability
respiratory function
thermal regulation
neurological function
pain
fatigue
sleep
nutrition and hydration
immune / infection state
sensory function
mobility
cognitive load
stress regulation
body-modification stability
microbiome / symbiosis
```

No domain is a universal proxy for “health.”

A person may have chronic pain and high competence. A person may appear physically intact while experiencing severe sleep deprivation, infection, grief, or cognitive overload.

# 2. Condition Types

Conditions may be:

```text
acute injury
chronic condition
infectious illness
environmental exposure
occupational harm
body-modification complication
pregnancy or reproductive health state
age-related change
sensory or mobility variation
psychological trauma
grief
moral injury
burnout
unknown or alien condition
```

Each condition should define:

```text
cause or uncertainty
observable signs
private symptoms
functional effects
likely course
care options
risks and tradeoffs
accommodations
social interpretation risks
recovery possibilities
```

# 3. Injury

Injury should create specific consequences rather than generic health loss.

Examples:

```text
hand injury affects grip, tool precision, and pain
rib injury affects breathing, exertion, and sleep
hearing injury changes communication and hazard perception
burn affects temperature, infection risk, movement, and care needs
concussion affects attention, memory confidence, and sensory tolerance
```

The player should be able to:

```text
stabilize
protect
transport
rest
adapt tools
seek diagnosis
accept or refuse treatment
rehabilitate
work within limits
```

Combat lethality and persistent injury are worldline-profile choices. The default representative build should prefer readable, recoverable harm over constant permanent maiming.

# 4. Illness and Public Health

Illness systems must avoid dehumanizing people as infection vectors.

Public health may include:

```text
water quality
ventilation
sanitation
vaccination or prophylaxis
quarantine
contact notification
food safety
occupational exposure
vector ecology
medical supply
trust and communication
```

Quarantine must define:

```text
evidence threshold
least-restrictive action
material support
appeal
review cadence
exit condition
privacy
anti-discrimination protection
```

A public-health response that protects people while abandoning those isolated is a failure.

# 5. Trauma and Stress

Trauma is not one meter and not every stressful event is trauma.

Possible trauma-related processes:

```text
heightened threat expectation
avoidance
intrusive memory
sleep disruption
body tension
numbness
guilt
shame
mistrust
sensory triggers
moral injury
grief
```

Responses vary by person, culture, support, meaning, prior history, and body.

Trauma should affect:

```text
attention
prediction
social trust
risk preference
memory salience
body expression
sleep
work capacity
relationship needs
```

It must not automatically:

```text
make a person violent
make testimony worthless
remove agency
create a puzzle for the player to “fix” through one conversation
```

# 6. Recovery

Recovery pathways may include:

```text
physical stabilization
rehabilitation
assistive technology
rest
safe routine
peer support
therapy or counseling
ritual and mourning
public acknowledgment
justice or restitution
changed work conditions
medication
body modification
environmental accommodation
new identity and meaning
```

Recovery can be:

```text
partial
nonlinear
relational
interrupted
transformative
stable without cure
```

The game should recognize successful adaptation as recovery even when a condition remains.

# 7. Care Relationships

Care may be provided by:

```text
self
household member
friend
community caregiver
clinician
medic
machine steward
animal companion
institution
alien symbiont
```

Care relationships must track:

```text
consent
competence
trust
privacy
burden
reciprocity
power
continuity
```

The person receiving care retains agency. Dependency does not imply ownership.

The caregiver also has limits, needs, rights, and possible burnout.

# 8. Medical Authority and Consent

Medical decisions may involve:

```text
informed consent
emergency necessity
advance directive
supported decision-making
guardianship with appeal
machine recommendation
public-health order
body-sovereignty law
```

Emergency treatment cannot become permanent authority by default.

Any invasive, identity-altering, reproductive, memory-affecting, or inheritable intervention requires stronger consent and review.

The existing body-sovereignty principle remains authoritative:

```text
A body that requires permission to keep functioning is not free.
```

# 9. Privacy

Health information has scopes:

```text
private to person
shared with named caregiver
clinical team
household emergency scope
public-health minimum
institutional accommodation need
public voluntary disclosure
```

Gameplay should often expose **functional needs without diagnosis**.

Example:

```text
“Requires low-noise work station and rest interval.”
```

Not:

```text
“NPC has hidden diagnosis X.”
```

# 10. Accommodations

Accommodations may include:

```text
ramps and lifts
seated workstations
alternate controls
captions and visual alerts
low-sensory rooms
rest schedules
protective equipment
prosthetics
mobility devices
remote participation
modified tools
care leave
accessible vehicles
```

They should alter real capability and participation.

A well-designed settlement becomes stronger because more people can contribute and live well—not because disabled people receive a morale bonus.

# 11. Death and Reconstitution

Health, death, and reconstitution must interoperate without erasing consequence.

Reconstitution may restore embodiment while leaving:

```text
source-chain uncertainty
memory loss
relationship disruption
body mismatch
medical dependency
trauma
public-health concern
```

It must not be framed as a universal cure. Some people may refuse reconstitution, request limits, or define continuity differently.

# 12. Player Experience and Accessibility

The player may choose reduced simulation for:

```text
pain audio/visual effects
blood and injury detail
trauma flashbacks
medical procedures
infection mechanics
care repetition
breathing effects
screen distortion
```

Accessibility settings must not secretly make the player morally worse or mechanically illegitimate.

Care gameplay should emphasize:

```text
observation
communication
stabilization
prioritization
transport
resource coordination
follow-up
relationship
```

It should avoid repetitive button-holding over suffering bodies as its primary fantasy.

# 13. Seedworks Representative Proof

A storm and convoy failure produce:

```text
one acute injury
one chronic mobility need
one exhausted caregiver
one ambiguous exposure concern
one NPC with trauma-linked avoidance
one inaccessible public meeting
one private medical fact
one accommodation intervention
```

The player cannot solve the situation through supplies alone.

Possible actions include:

```text
stabilize and transport
change work schedules
repair an accessible route
protect privacy
improve ventilation
provide respite
challenge coercive quarantine
support a public acknowledgment
```

Success is not “all meters full.” Success is that people retain agency, care capacity becomes sustainable, avoidable harm decreases, and the settlement learns.

# 14. Acceptance Criteria

- Health domains are specific and cause-linked.
- Diagnosis remains uncertain until evidence supports it.
- Private medical state is not exposed through ordinary UI, rumor, or generated dialogue.
- Trauma affects behavior without scripting inevitable violence or incapacity.
- Accommodations alter real interaction and participation.
- Caregiver burden and consent are represented.
- Refusal and supported decision-making are playable.
- Recovery can succeed without full cure.
- Public-health restrictions have support, appeal, and exit conditions.
- Accessibility settings reduce distress and repetition without narrative punishment.
- Death and reconstitution preserve body-sovereignty boundaries.
- The deterministic baseline works without external models.

# Final Rule

> **Health should make bodies matter, care visible, and recovery meaningful—without teaching that a person is only whole when every wound disappears.**
