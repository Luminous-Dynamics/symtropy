# Canon Registry and Document Governance

## Purpose

This document identifies the current canonical decision spine and integrates the v2.3 player-founded civilization campaign without confusing authored design with implemented capability.

## Status Vocabulary

- `canonical`: controlling design law for its owned question;
- `canonical-draft`: approved direction requiring implementation evidence;
- `implementation-spec`: technical or production contract awaiting code or proof;
- `supporting`: useful context that does not override canon;
- `historical`: retained history rather than current direction;
- `superseded`: replaced by a named successor;
- `unclassified`: not yet migrated into the metadata system.

# v2.3 Canonical Additions

## Player-Founded Civilization

[Player-Founded Civilization, Settlement Legacy, and Worldline Contract](./PLAYER_FOUNDED_CIVILIZATION_SETTLEMENT_LEGACY_AND_WORLDLINE_CONTRACT_V0_1.md) owns:

- collective founding;
- prior claims;
- player roles and founder limits;
- rights floor;
- charter amendability;
- succession;
- absence;
- contested legacy;
- worldline ancestry.

Its governing rule is:

> **The player may help begin a civilization. The civilization must be able to continue, reinterpret, reject, or outgrow them.**

## Settlement Content Standard

[Player-Founded Settlement Campaign Packet Standard](../ops/PLAYER_FOUNDED_SETTLEMENT_CAMPAIGN_PACKET_STANDARD_V0_1.md) controls authored and procedural packet requirements.

A founding packet is invalid when it contains buildings without prior inhabitants, public services without labor, charters without distributional effects, or successors that exist only to validate the founder.

## Civic Runtime

[Settlement Founding, Charter, Institution, and Public Service Runtime](../tech/SETTLEMENT_FOUNDING_CHARTER_INSTITUTION_AND_PUBLIC_SERVICE_RUNTIME_V0_1.md) owns authoritative civic state:

```text
claims
participants
compacts
charters
institutions
offices
public services
budgets
legitimacy
succession
multiplayer authority
```

The runtime does not confer government authority through construction ownership or host status.

## Emergent Campaign Runtime

[Emergent Campaign Detection, Causal Storylet, and Player History Runtime](../tech/EMERGENT_CAMPAIGN_DETECTION_CAUSAL_STORYLET_AND_PLAYER_HISTORY_RUNTIME_V0_1.md) owns the conversion of authoritative systemic causality into legible opportunities.

It may detect and schedule meaningful pressure. It may not injure, betray, expose, or kill people merely to refresh engagement.

## Promise and Legacy Runtime

[Player Promise, Office, Reputation, and Legacy Runtime](../tech/PLAYER_PROMISE_OFFICE_REPUTATION_AND_LEGACY_RUNTIME_V0_1.md) owns commitments, office tenure, audience-specific reputation, attribution, founder myth, inheritance, death, and reconstitution consequences.

No global morality, loyalty, or founder-importance scalar is canonical.

## Place and Culture

[Player-Built Places, Architectural Memory, and Landmark Content Bible](../lore/PLAYER_BUILT_PLACES_ARCHITECTURAL_MEMORY_AND_LANDMARK_CONTENT_BIBLE_V0_1.md) controls how structures become places through use and time.

[Player-Created Culture, Ritual, Art, and Symbol Runtime](../tech/PLAYER_CREATED_CULTURE_RITUAL_ART_AND_SYMBOL_RUNTIME_V0_1.md) controls cultural proposal, adoption, mutation, rejection, commercialization, and transmission.

Player designation does not create a landmark. Player creation does not force cultural adoption.

## Return and Succession

[Returning Player, Absence, Inheritance, and Successor Campaign Packets](../lore/RETURNING_PLAYER_ABSENCE_INHERITANCE_AND_SUCCESSOR_CAMPAIGN_PACKETS_V0_1.md) governs authored return experiences.

The returning founder may encounter expired authority, renamed places, improved successors, legitimate amendment, disputed property, founder myths, or historical insignificance.

# Authority Hierarchy

When documents conflict, resolve in this order:

1. safety and rights constraints;
2. authoritative world-state and multiplayer truth rules;
3. current canonical contracts;
4. current implementation specifications;
5. production standards and benchmarks;
6. lore and content bibles;
7. supporting and historical documents.

A newer version supersedes an older version only when metadata names the relationship or the registry explicitly states it.

# v2.3 Authority Boundaries

## Building Is Not Ownership

Construction provenance may create property, contract, repayment, or authorship claims. It does not grant ownership over residents, government, public voice, or survival services.

## Founding Is Not Empty-Land Discovery

Site selection must evaluate prior residency, seasonal use, displacement, watershed effects, machine stewardship, nonhuman habitat, public easements, and treaty restrictions.

## Emergency Is Not Permanent Sovereignty

Emergency authority requires scope, expiry, review, record, appeal, and transition.

## Charters Are Not Perk Menus

Clauses alter rights, burdens, administration, services, and conflict. They must be amendable and reviewable.

## Culture Is Not Player Customization

The player may create and propose. Communities adopt, mutate, reject, commercialize, or forget.

## Legacy Is Not Reputation XP

Legacy arises from traces, institutions, harms, benefits, records, and interpretation. Fame is neither required nor universally positive.

## Return Is Not Restoration of Control

Verified identity does not automatically restore office, credentials, property, consent, relationships, or historical interpretation.

## Design Is Not Implementation

All v2.3 features remain **D3 / I0** until promoted by code and evidence.

# Required Production Proof

Promotion requires the [Twenty-Year Player-Founded Region Benchmark](../ops/TWENTY_YEAR_PLAYER_FOUNDED_REGION_BENCHMARK_V0_1.md), including:

- prior claims;
- twelve residents and four households;
- three compact paths;
- five or more public services;
- one charter and amendment;
- office succession;
- non-player initiative;
- cultural mutation;
- landmark emergence;
- five-year absence;
- founder death or reconstitution;
- successor government;
- multiplayer authority safety;
- worldline fork;
- deterministic replay.

# Canonical Summary

> **Symtropy permits players to found places worth loving, but love, labor, and construction do not make other people property. A civilization is proven when it can carry history beyond the player.**
