# ANIMA Fidelity and Biography Continuity V0

Status: normative Living World integration contract. Documentation only.

## Purpose

Bind embodied-agent biography and cognitive continuity to the existing Living World population/refinement authority rather than defining a separate AI LOD system.

## Parent authority

The Living World population/refinement contracts remain authoritative for Level P/A/I semantics, information sufficiency, transition admissibility, promotion/collapse, and one-owner rules.

ANIMA contributes an information census describing which cognitive/social/body/action fields remain future-bearing.

## Core invariant

> Any individual state that changes future canonical behavior is process information; representation changes may discard it only when the destination remains qualified as sufficient for all surviving semantics.

Distance, camera, renderer LOD, FPS, CPU/GPU load, or entity unload cannot independently authorize semantic information loss.

## Biography-bearing state

Examples that may require Level I persistent identity or an equivalently sufficient enriched representation include:

- individually learned fear/avoidance;
- companion or named identity;
- parent/offspring or stable pair relation;
- partner-specific communication convention;
- important injury/chronic impairment;
- active committed action with unsettled future effects;
- persistent territory/route/place knowledge;
- relationship expectations tied to a particular partner;
- synthetic self-model/fault history tied to a particular robot.

This list is process-relative, not a requirement to persist every organism forever.

## Information census

Every future-bearing ANIMA field should declare:

1. canonical owner;
2. individual/group/aggregate scope;
3. consuming processes;
4. whether loss changes future semantics;
5. exact or qualified/toleranced preservation requirement;
6. admissible transform class under Living World fidelity authority;
7. persistence/serialization form;
8. canonical expiry/forgetting policy where applicable.

## Identity-critical invariants

Identity links, action/effect settlement identities, body-region injury identity, source provenance required by retained memory, and partner identity are not weakened by numerical tolerance.

A tolerance may apply to a bounded quantitative observable; it does not permit ownership, provenance, count, or identity drift.

## Collapse

Conceptually:

`active state -> process/information sufficiency -> qualified transition or reject/enrich`

A horse with rider-specific history, dog with learned partner convention, deer with future-relevant injury, or robot with active fault-dependent task cannot become an exchangeable anonymous member if the destination representation cannot preserve the semantics their enabled processes consume.

## Promotion

Promotion cannot fabricate exact historical biography absent from the coarse source.

A richer representation being required does not make missing history reconstructible.

Retained exact known state, deterministic conditional derivation, and later measurement/assimilation remain distinct provenance classes under Living World authority.

Deterministic invention is not historical knowledge.

## Forgetting vs demotion

Canonical forgetting/consolidation is an ANIMA cognitive transition under explicit policy/time.

Representation demotion is a fidelity transition that must preserve all surviving semantics.

Demotion is never a substitute for forgetting.

## Active action continuity

Committed/executing actions retain enough information through save/reload, region transfer, and fidelity transition to prevent:

- duplicate ecological/resource effects;
- lost commitments/reservations;
- replayed completed work;
- forgotten interruption state;
- target identity aliasing.

## Group/cohort state

Anonymous collective state may remain aggregate where no individual relation/history is process-relevant.

A private memory in one individual does not automatically become group knowledge during collapse.

Genuinely group-level learned state requires its own qualified provenance and authority semantics.

## Qualification direction

At minimum test:

1. repeated promote/demote preserves exact identity links and declared observables without derived-to-known ratcheting;
2. individual learned fear blocks an insufficient exchangeable collapse;
3. partner-specific relationship blocks collapse that would lose partner identity;
4. active action survives transition with exactly-once effects;
5. persistent injury still restricts capability after promotion;
6. group fission/fusion/region transfer cannot duplicate persistent individuals;
7. promotion from history-poor aggregate state leaves unavailable exact biography unknown;
8. save while coarse, reload, then promote matches uninterrupted qualified path for declared observables;
9. presentation LOD alone cannot change authority level or biography;
10. hardware/load pressure cannot choose semantically insufficient fidelity.

## Performance principle

The optimization target is minimum sufficient information:

- population/cohort state when sufficient;
- group closure when sufficient;
- temporary active refinement when processes require it;
- persistent individual identity only when future semantics require continuity.

This contract does not prescribe camera-distance AI quality tiers.

## Non-goals

No parallel ANIMA LOD ladder, permanent detailed identity for every organism, invented history on promotion, demotion-as-forgetting, or weakening of Living World exact authority/transition requirements is introduced here.
