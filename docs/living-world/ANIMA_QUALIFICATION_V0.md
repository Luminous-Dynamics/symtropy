# ANIMA Qualification V0

Status: normative Living World integration contract. Documentation only.

## Purpose

Define falsifiable qualification for embodied-agent causality and liveness without collapsing causal correctness, biological plausibility, presentation quality, and subjective player experience into one claim.

## Parent observatory

The Living World Observatory evidence ladder remains authoritative. This contract specializes it for ANIMA.

## Evidence ladder

### L0 — static authority/schema

Dependency graph, ownership, identities, closed/versioned schemas, forbidden authority paths, and test fixtures are reviewable.

### L1 — exact execution

Exact product/toolchain/scenario identities execute. Replay properties pass where exact semantics are claimed.

### L2 — differential/metamorphic controls

One causal factor changes while relevant hidden truth/current state is controlled. Only admitted downstream observables should change.

### L3 — causal behavior

Longer scenarios demonstrate history, uncertainty, autonomy, social propagation, physiology/body capability, and persistence through legitimate paths.

### L4 — presentation legibility

Animation/audio/body language makes canonical causes readable without inventing or modifying canonical behavior.

### L5 — human playtest

A human can judge coherence, readability, individuality, and perceived aliveness. L5 does not upgrade unsupported mechanical, cognitive, or biological claims.

## Required hostile families

### Non-omniscience

Hold a hidden target/event fixed while varying no emission, incompatible receptor, occluded/masked path, clear qualified path, and stale evidence.

No source-attributable percept/belief is allowed without an admitted evidence path.

### Source-semantic leakage

Hold raw carrier evidence equal while varying hidden semantic cause. Raw sensing cannot reveal source meaning that the receptor did not receive.

### History sensitivity

Hold present external state equal while varying qualified prior experience. Later belief/action may differ only through retained history.

### Uncertainty and epistemic action

Reduce confidence while preserving task relevance. Agents may orient, sniff/sample, test footing, inspect, scan, wait/listen, probe, or otherwise seek information. The action consumes real time and can fail.

### Request non-authority

Apply the same external request under different qualified physiology, risk, relationship, or capability conditions. Accept/hesitate/seek-information/alternative/refuse/unable must be causally distinguishable where modeled.

### Social learning vs shared environment

Distinguish direct learner, qualified observer, and same-environment/no-demonstration control before claiming observational learning.

### No telepathy

One agent detects a threat. Other agents may update only after receiving a qualified signal/observation or through an explicitly declared group-level closure.

### Relationship specificity

Pair-specific history may change response to the same current partner cue. A global social scalar cannot substitute where partner identity is part of the claim.

### Body-relative affordance

Present equivalent observed geometry to different bodies/capability states. Affordance sets may differ for causal embodiment reasons.

### Persistence/fidelity

Compare uninterrupted execution, save/reload, region transfer, unload/reload, and repeated qualified promotion/demotion. Future-bearing identity, memory, relationship, injury, action, and effect-settlement semantics must remain compatible.

### Presentation non-authority

Vary renderer presence, FPS, presentation LOD, animation/IK detail, and audio. Canonical sensing/belief/intent/action may not change solely because presentation changed.

### Synthetic non-privilege

A simulated robot receives only qualified sensors/body telemetry. It may not obtain exact hidden entity class, position, pathability, damage, or threat state merely because the simulator knows it.

### Player absence

Remove the player for a bounded long scenario. Claimed autonomous agents continue coherent regulation, routine, social, ecological, or operational behavior rather than meaningless random wandering.

## Cross-species theorem

A flagship integrated qualification should demonstrate:

`private evidence in agent A -> observable emission/expression -> raw percept in agent B -> uncertain interpretation/belief -> novel epistemic/action intent in B`

Agent B must not share A's private state or the hidden target variable.

## Decision/action evidence

Qualification builds may emit bounded provenance records referencing stable IDs/hashes for:

- percepts;
- memories/beliefs;
- appraisal/regulation state;
- considered affordances;
- request/relationship context;
- selected intent/disposition;
- action transitions/interruption reasons;
- settled effects.

Evidence tooling is not player mind-reading UI and must not become canonical behavior authority.

## Exact vs toleranced semantics

Each fixture declares which properties are exact and which are toleranced.

Identity, provenance, ownership, no-emission/no-percept invariants, exactly-once settlement, and canonical ordering where specified cannot be waived by numerical tolerance.

Locomotor/contact values or alternate propagation backend outputs may use qualified tolerances when the contract declares them.

## Claim separation

Do not use one `realistic AI` PASS. Track separately:

- causal correctness;
- replay/determinism correctness;
- ethological/engineering plausibility;
- presentation legibility;
- subjective liveness.

Passing one does not establish the others.

## Initial reference beings

The first architecture is validated against distinct reference demands:

- horse: riding, contact, history, agency;
- dog: olfaction, learned partner communication, self-initiated companionship;
- deer: player-independent prey ecology and non-telepathic group information;
- synthetic/Symthaea embodiment: typed sensors, self-model, controller/safety feedback, active sensing.

No species explosion is required before these references work.

## Initial integrated cell

A compact deterministic cell should exercise controlled crossing history, local scent tracking, deer alarm propagation, synthetic cross-species inference, conflicting evidence, player absence, save/reload mid-action, and fidelity transition attacks.

Correctness and evidence precede open-world scale and presentation polish.

## Non-goals

This contract does not establish consciousness, universal biological realism, veterinary fidelity, humanlike theory of mind, or subjective sentience. It defines how narrower implementation claims are tested without hidden shortcuts.
