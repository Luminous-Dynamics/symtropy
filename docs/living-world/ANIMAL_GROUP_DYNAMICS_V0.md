# Animal Group Dynamics V0

Status: normative Living World design contract. Documentation only.

## Purpose

Define herds, flocks, schools, packs, colonies, nurseries, and migration groups as multiscale ecological/social state without forcing every collective process into either one giant agent or millions of permanently individualized agents.

## Core invariant

> Group state may be canonical where collective organization changes future behavior, but it cannot erase individual continuity that an enabled process still requires.

## Candidate group state

A group may retain information such as:

- group scope/identity;
- species/population binding;
- membership count and optionally stable member identities;
- centroid/home range;
- cohesion/alignment/separation parameters;
- migration objective/route state;
- alarm/arousal state;
- leadership/dominance structure where modeled;
- nursery/offspring composition;
- territory/den/nest association;
- shared memory/learned route where biologically justified.

## Individual vs group authority

Some processes are group-primary:

- flock/school alignment;
- herd migration;
- collective alarm;
- anonymous swarm cohesion.

Others require particular individuals:

- parent/offspring bond;
- stable pair bond;
- named companion;
- dominance relation;
- injury of a known leader where leadership continuity matters.

The representation must match the process requirement rather than forcing one ontology.

## Formation / fission / fusion

Joining, leaving, splitting, and merging groups are canonical social/ecological transitions.

They must preserve membership/count authority exactly and avoid duplicate members when fidelity or region boundaries change.

A group identifier is not a substitute for population biomass/count ownership.

## Spatial structure

Group movement should preserve biologically important structure where needed:

- formation/cohesion;
- predator-prey shielding;
- nursery placement;
- leader/follower relationships;
- front/back exposure;
- school/flock orientation.

A single centroid is insufficient whenever within-group arrangement affects future processes.

## Perception / signaling

Group behavior may amplify or aggregate signals such as alarms, movement noise, scent, or visual displays.

Individuals still require a causal route to receive/update group state unless a qualified group-level closure directly models the collective process.

## Coarse / active refinement

A distant herd may remain group/cohort state. Near the player it may refine into active individuals while conserving group membership/count and preserving required social correlations.

Active member realization cannot create more members than the group/population authority owns.

## Qualification direction

At minimum test:

1. group fission/fusion conserves membership exactly;
2. refinement/demotion cannot duplicate members;
3. anonymous collective state can remain aggregate where individual relationships are irrelevant;
4. parent/offspring or stable-leader relations block collapse to a representation that would lose them;
5. region-boundary migration preserves one canonical group, not two copies;
6. input container/thread order cannot change collective membership authority;
7. group alarm/emission remains replay deterministic for a fixed scheme;
8. group centroid-only closure is rejected where within-group spatial structure affects enabled processes.

## Non-goals

This contract does not prescribe one flocking algorithm, pack hierarchy model, swarm intelligence architecture, or universal social biology.
