## Major Product Corrections

- Added **lived civilizational agency** as the central product definition.
- Added delight, play, and self-expression as a protected pillar.
- Added an anti-monopoly rule so water, governance, factories, combat, the Field Deck, or any other subsystem cannot consume the whole game.
- Defined session rhythms across expeditions, construction, convoys, combat, research, culture, civic play, and recovery.
- Defined a friction budget and explicit anti-chore rules.
- Connected progression to embodied, technical, infrastructural, relational, and epistemic capability instead of universal XP.
- Defined cross-system event ownership and authority layers.
- Staged system complexity across the first ten hours.
- Added content, animation, equipment, vehicle, NPC, threat, economy, cultural, performance, and cut budgets for the representative build.
- Replaced the early prototype checklist as the current playtest owner with a complete research program.
